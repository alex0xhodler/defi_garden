How the Math Works

Monthly compounding of a recurring deposit (annuity-due): FV = P × ((1 + r)ⁿ − 1) / r × (1 + r), where P is the monthly deposit, r = APY ÷ 12, and n = years × 12. DeFi APYs change daily — the planner re-pulls live rates on every visit, which is why the table above is labeled illustrative.

Risk & Reality

DeFi yields are variable and can fall to zero. Pools carry smart contract, oracle, depeg, and liquidation risk. Nothing here is a bank deposit; no FDIC/SIPC protection applies. Education, not financial advice.
