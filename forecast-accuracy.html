Yield Forecast Accuracy: Empirical Track Record

DeFi Garden Underwriting Research · Evaluated against immutable daily snapshots · Horizon: 14 Days · Education, not financial advice.

DeFi Garden evaluates 14-day forward yield forecast distributions ([p10, p90]) against realized outcomes across single-sided lending venues, vaults, and staking markets (excluding volatile AMM LP pairs). Under our core operating principle — honest numbers beat exciting numbers — every run benchmarks predictions recorded on the exact date of emission against a naive "no-change" baseline (today's APY).

Key Research Finding: Across 22 resolved daily test runs, the empirical 80% confidence interval captures 71.0% of actual outcomes. However, the median forecast skill score sits at -0.01: in our first 10 test runs, assuming today's rate holds was more accurate than this forecast. Roughly 60% of lending yields barely moved (<0.1 pp), which the model handled well, but on pools with meaningful rate movement (≥0.5 pp), assuming no change was substantially closer.
