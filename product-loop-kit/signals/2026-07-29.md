## NEW FINDING → 173 (P1/READY 9.0): 1,749 of 2,200 SEO pages send the reader into an empty grid
Every generated SEO page ends with a primary CTA into the app. Measured against **live DefiLlama**:

| surface | pages | primary CTA returns 0 pools | share |
|---|---|---|---|
| `chains/*.html` | 88 | **48** | 54.5% |
| `tokens/*.html` | 2,112 | **1,701** | 80.5% |
| **total** | **2,200** | **1,749** | **79.5%** |

Root cause is a **floor mismatch between a page and its own link**, not a data problem:
`generate-token-pages.js:60` `const MIN_POOL_TVL = 100000;` and `generate-chain-pages.js:65`
(`isQualifyingPool` — "trust rail + $100K floor, one gate") decide what a page lists, while `app.js:801`
`DEFAULT_MIN_TVL = 10000000` decides what the CTA's target shows. 100×.

Verbatim from `chains/cardano.html` (18,320 bytes, live on prod):
> Cardano's largest live pool is liqwid (ADA) at 2.16% ($6.13M TVL). **33 pools** across 31 tokens clear
> DeFi Garden's $100K TVL floor, $20.36M in total. … **See live pools on Cardano →**

The button goes to `https://www.defi.garden/?chain=Cardano`. Live pool count at the app's default floor:
**0**. The page names 33 pools and the click delivers none — and the largest of them ($6.13M) could not
clear $10M even in principle.

Pages that took crawler traffic **this week**: `/chains/linea` → CTA returns **1**; `/chains/aptos` → **1**;
`/chains/plasma` → **10**; `/chains/ethereum` → **406**. So the defect is skewed to exactly the long tail
where SEO is supposed to pay off.

**Fix hypothesis, proven by measurement this tick** — have the generator emit the floor its own page was
built with. `app.js:927` already honors it: *"Respect explicit `minTvl=0`; fall back to `DEFAULT_MIN_TVL`
only when param is absent."*

| CTA | default | `&minTvl=100000` |
|---|---|---|
| `?chain=Cardano` | 0 | **33** (page claims 33 — exact) |
| `?chain=Celo` | 0 | 16 |
| `?chain=Fantom` | 0 | 6 |
| `?token=1INCH` | 0 | 17 |
| `?token=ACX` | 0 | 2 |

**This does not touch the NEVER list.** `DEFAULT_MIN_TVL` is unchanged; the rail's *default* is untouched;
the link merely states the floor the page already published. `?minTvl=` is an existing routed param
(`home.html:77`) already emitted across the sitemap. It is the unblocked slice of **133**'s territory —
133 is BLOCKED on a human decision about changing *app discovery behavior at the floor*; 173 changes only
what a generated link says about itself.
