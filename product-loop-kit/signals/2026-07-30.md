## NEW FINDING → 182 (P1/READY 8.4): half the north star can silently disappear, and nothing measures it
The audit's P1 is on `ae6b7add` (uniswap-v4 PAXG-ILMU, Polygon, $13.98M TVL). **Checked before ticketing,
because the playbook's fixture-trap rule demands it — and the check changed the finding rather than
killing it:**

`PoolDetail.js:583` and `:1662` render the CTA as `protocolUrlWithRef && React.createElement(...)`. The URL
resolves through `app.js:2429` `getProtocolUrl()`: `pool.url` → `dynamicProtocolUrls[…]` → static
`PROTOCOL_URLS[…]` → `null`. Against **live `api.llama.fi/protocols` (7,962 protocols, 5,181 url keys,
fetched in-session)**, `uniswap-v4` **does** resolve (`https://app.uniswap.org/`). So the audit's finding
is not "this pool is broken in prod" — it is the **degraded path**, reproduced faithfully because the
harness blocks that host. That is the risk, not an artifact:

- `dynamicProtocolUrls` comes from a **second, different third-party endpoint** (`api.llama.fi/protocols`,
  not the pools API), fetched at runtime 100ms after mount (`app.js:~1290`), and **failing silently by
  design** (`app.js:1285` — *"Fail silently - static PROTOCOL_URLS will be used as fallback"*).
- The static fallback has **96 entries**. Measured against today's 741-pool snapshot, that covers
  **525 pools (70.9%)** and leaves **216 pools (29.1%), $8.9B TVL, 134 distinct projects** with **no CTA at
  all** whenever the fetch fails — ad-blocker, outage, CSP, slow first paint, or a user who bounces before
  it lands. Biggest uncovered: `uniswap-v4` (10 pools), `pendle` (8), `aave-v4` (6), `orca-dex` (5),
  `nest-credit` (5), `navi-lending` (5), `liquity-v2` (4).
- **On the path our actual traffic takes it is worse.** The SEO estate deep-links **3,677 distinct
  `?pool=` ids**; of the 438 resolvable in today's snapshot, **148 (33.8%)** sit on projects with no static
  entry. So a third of the pool-detail pages the landers point at lose half their conversion surface on the
  degraded path.
- **The result is cached permanently** (`localStorage['defi-protocols']`), so the **first visit decides** —
  and first-visit-from-search is precisely the cohort the SEO estate exists to serve.
- When it fails the page renders **nothing at all** — no fallback link, no explanation, no DefiLlama pool
  page. The reader cannot tell a missing button from a protocol we chose not to link.
- **One pool is genuinely unreachable even on the happy path:** project `sdai` ($61M) resolves to no URL
  from live-dynamic *or* static. Its detail page permanently shows one of two north-star CTAs.
- **Item 138 already fixed exactly one instance of this by hand** (`sky-lending`), and
  `test_protocol_cta_fallback.js` guards that one protocol with a 2-fixture test. **The class was never
  closed and nothing measures its coverage.** This is the "checker follows the last bug" pattern
  (148 → 159 → 166 → 173, item 175's thesis) in its *repair* costume rather than its detector costume: the
  fix, not just the detector, was scoped to the single instance that bit us.
