# SPEC 258 — Perp Funding Yield Data Engine (`src/perps-fetcher.js`)
**Parent**: Item 258 in `product-loop-kit/BACKLOG.md` | **Risk Tier**: HIGH | **Status**: READY

## 1. Goal
Build a zero-dependency, lightweight fetcher module (`src/perps-fetcher.js`) that retrieves live perp funding rates, open interest, mark prices, and 24h volume from Hyperliquid (`api.hyperliquid.xyz/info`), Lighter, Drift, and GMX APIs, computing annualized Short and Long Funding Yield APYs.

## 2. Evidence
- On-chain traders search for funding rates and market bias on volatile assets (Hyperliquid OIL, Lighter SPCX, HYPE, SOL, ETH, BTC).
- Holding a short or long position when funding rates are active generates daily/annualized funding yields ($\text{Annualized APY} = \text{Hourly Rate} \times 24 \times 365$).

## 3. Implementation Plan
1. **Module `src/perps-fetcher.js`**:
   - `fetchHyperliquidPerps()`: Sends POST to `https://api.hyperliquid.xyz/info` with body `{ type: "metaAndAssetCtxs" }`.
   - Parses 230+ universe markets (name, hourly funding rate, openInterest, markPx, dayNtlVlm).
   - Computes:
     - `longFundingApy = -hourlyFunding * 24 * 365 * 100`
     - `shortFundingApy = hourlyFunding * 24 * 365 * 100`
     - `bias = shortFundingApy > 0 ? 'SHORTS_EARN' : 'LONGS_EARN'`
2. **Snapshot Caching**:
   - Save output artifact to `data/perps-snapshot.json`.
3. **Acceptance Tests**:
   - `test_perps_fetcher.js`: Assert `fetchHyperliquidPerps()` returns valid array of markets with non-null funding APY, mark price, and volume.

## 4. Verification
- `node test_perps_fetcher.js` passes.
- Execute dry-run logging top 10 Hyperliquid perp funding yield opportunities.
