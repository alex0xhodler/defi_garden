## What (verbatim from the 058 study's drafted item, now promoted)

A `generate-pools-snapshot.js` CI generator producing a trust-rail-filtered, 13-field snapshot
(+ per-chain/per-token slices) with a `generatedAt` freshness stamp, wired into `sitemap-update.yml`;
FE (`app.js`/`planner.js`/`PoolDetail.js`) reads the snapshot first and falls back to a direct
`yields.llama.fi` fetch when the snapshot is stale (>15 min) or missing.

The 13 used fields (058 §0): pool, chain, project, symbol, tvlUsd, apyBase, apyReward, apyMean30d,
poolMeta, url, exposure, ilRisk, underlyingTokens.
