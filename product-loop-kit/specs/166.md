# Spec 166: the AI-discovery surface's links must go where they claim — and pool rows must deep-link to pool-detail

## Evidence

All figures fetched from **live prod by curl on 2026-07-28**, not read out of the checkout.
(`signals/2026-07-28.md`, findings section 166.)

**Class 1 — 15 pool rows link to the bare homepage.** `generate-llms.js:606`:

```js
const poolUrl = pool.url || meta.baseUrl;
```

No DefiLlama payload has a `url` field. `data/pools-snapshot.json` record keys are
`pool, chain, project, symbol, tvlUsd, apyBase, apyReward, apyMean30d, poolMeta, exposure, ilRisk,
underlyingTokens, kpis`; the live `/pools` shape adds `apy`, not `url`. So the `|| meta.baseUrl` fallback
fires on **100% of rows, always**, and has for the life of the surface. Live prod, verbatim
(`llms-full.txt`, "Live High-Yield Opportunities (by Chain)", lines 4994-4997):

```
- uniswap-v3 · WETH-USDC — 95.55% APY, $110,825,218 TVL — https://www.defi.garden
- uniswap-v3 · WETH-USDC — 31.72% APY, $10,187,107 TVL — https://www.defi.garden
- uniswap-v3 · WETH-CBBTC — 26.41% APY, $10,998,904 TVL — https://www.defi.garden
- aerodrome-v1 · USDC-AERO — 19.47% APY, $26,142,073 TVL — https://www.defi.garden
```

`grep -cE "TVL — https://www\.defi\.garden$" llms-full.txt` → **15**, across 5 chain sections
(Base, Solana, Ethereum, Arbitrum, Monad).

**Class 2 — 17 `?search=` links drop the query.** 7 in `llms.txt` (6 protocol rows at lines 47-52 + the
`"Pendle opportunities"` example at line 59), 10 in `llms-full.txt` (lines 4957-4966). Emitted by
`generate-llms.js:474` (query example) and `:577` (`protocolUrl`). `search` is **not** in
`home.html:77` `ANALYTICS_PARAMS` (`['token','chain','pool','poolTypes','protocols','minTvl','minApy','app']`)
nor in `:78` `PLANNER_PARAMS`, so the IA router sets `__APP_MODE = 'landing'`; `landing.js` reads only `lang`
(`landing.js:52`). Live example: `https://www.defi.garden/?search=lido` → the search-first landing with an
empty box. **The correct param already exists and is already routed: `?protocols=<slug>`.**

**Class 3 — 8 top-yield rows are not pool-specific.** `generate-llms.js:492` builds
`?token=<symbol>&chain=<chain>`. That resolves (`app.js:835` — `poolSymbol.toUpperCase().includes(token)`),
so these are not dead links, but they cannot address a pool. Consequence, live in `llms.txt` lines 67 and 71:
two distinct Base uniswap-v3 WETH-USDC pools — **95.5% APY / $110,825,218 TVL** and **31.7% /
$10,187,107 TVL** — render as two rows pointing at **one identical URL**, reading as contradictory claims
about a single page.

**The through-line.** `grep -c "?pool=" llms.txt llms-full.txt` → **0** and **0**. Every payload record
carries `pool` (the id). `/?pool=<id>` is the north-star surface per the 2026-07-23 standing decision. The
AI-discovery surface has never once linked to it.

## Hypothesis

We believe **pointing every pool row at `/?pool=<id>` and every protocol row at `/?protocols=<slug>`** will
**convert AI-referred intent into pool-detail arrivals (`pool_view{source=url_direct}`) and therefore into
north-star CTA clicks**, because today an LLM that cites one of our own pools has no way to send its reader
to that pool — the link resolves to the planner homepage (class 1), a query-less landing (class 2), or a
grid view that cannot distinguish the pool it just quoted (class 3). AI discovery is the only growth channel
that needs no human posting, and `llms.txt` is the artifact built to serve it.

Confidence: **supported** for the defect (verified verbatim on live prod; root cause is two lines of code).
**Guess** for the traffic effect — there is no measurable AI-referral traffic yet (`src` breakdown 14d:
one row, `undefined` = 47). This ships as a correctness fix whose upside is unmeasurable today; see
Measurement.

## Change

In `generate-llms.js` only:

1. **Add a pool-URL helper** that returns `${meta.baseUrl}/?pool=${encodeURIComponent(pool.pool)}` when
   `pool.pool` is a non-empty string, and falls back to the existing
   `?token=<symbol>&chain=<chain>` form when it is absent. Use it for **both** pool-row emitters:
   `:606` (the by-chain rows in `llms-full.txt`, replacing `pool.url || meta.baseUrl`) and `:492` (the
   "Current Top Yields" rows in `llms.txt`).
2. **Replace `?search=<protocol>` with `?protocols=<protocol>`** at `:577`, and change the `:474` query
   example from `?search=Pendle` to a routed equivalent (`?protocols=pendle`). Do not invent a `search`
   param in the router — the fix is to stop emitting a param that does not exist.
3. **Export the helper** so a test can call it directly (house pattern: `pickHighYield`, `APY_SANITY_LIMIT`,
   `MIN_TVL_USD`, `formatTvlFloor` are already exported for `test_llms_rails.js`).
4. Regenerate `llms.txt` + `llms-full.txt` via `node generate-llms.js` and commit the regenerated files —
   they are generator output, never hand-edited (CLAUDE.md / NORTH_STAR HIGH list).

**Explicitly OUT of scope:**
- Any change to `home.html`'s router, `ANALYTICS_PARAMS`, or `landing.js`. Adding `search` as a real param is
  a separate, larger idea; this item only stops emitting a link that cannot work.
- Deduplicating the two same-symbol rows. Once each row carries its own `?pool=<id>` the duplicate URL
  disappears by construction; two rows for two real pools is correct and honest.
- The planner's absence from the surface — that is item **168**, shipped separately.
- A link-integrity signal in `audit-app.js` — that is item **169**.
- `sitemap-*.xml`, `og/*.png`, `stories/`, token/chain page generators. Untouched.

- Trap: `generate-llms.js` reads the **live payload shape** (it uses `pool.apy`), while
  `data/pools-snapshot.json` has no `apy` field. Any fixture must be live-shaped or `pickHighYield()`

  `playbooks/dual-source-logic-divergence.md`.
- CI regenerates both files daily (`.github/workflows/sitemap-update.yml:110`), so the committed artifacts
  will be overwritten by the next run. The generator fix is what persists — assert on the generator AND on
  the artifact, and expect the artifact leg to be re-proved by the next CI commit.
