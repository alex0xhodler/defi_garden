# Spec 275 (renumbered from 267 — same collision): Dead-pool page bounces visitors instead of converting them — redesign the recovery path

## Evidence
Human live report + screenshot, 2026-08-12 session, verbatim: "dead pool page is so bad it will
bounce people immediately instead of converting them to try again or search/find/discover something
else that might be interesting since they were looking at tokens and yields."
Context: the page IS honest and functional (253 restored the heading; 012/072 provide the
alternatives grid; the 08-09 crawler click proves the rescue grid is at least mechanically clickable)
— the human's verdict is about CONVERSION QUALITY, not correctness. The visitor's known intent at
this moment: they wanted a specific pool's yield. The page must redirect that intent, not just
apologize.

## Hypothesis
A visitor landing on a dead `?pool=` link converts better when the page leads with what they can DO
next (search this token's family, browse similar high-quality pools, one-tap into the closest live
alternative) than when it leads with a full-width apology heading. Confidence: guess (no traffic to
measure; this is the human's product judgment, which is the binding signal pre-traffic).
- Instance of: dead-end-for-valid-query class (playbooks/product-audit.md class 2).
- Class closed by this item: no — this is one surface; the empty-state family (search-no-results,
  chain-no-results) should inherit whatever pattern wins, ticketed after the human approves the
  direction.
