# Spec 007: The arrival moment — greet share-link recipients and convert them

## Evidence
The north star is `share_link_opened → plan_created`, and its second step has no designed moment: a recipient opening a shared URL lands in the planner with zero acknowledgment that someone sent them this. `share_link_opened` fires on decode (planner.js:3293) but adoption is unframed and unprompted. Funnel step 2 conversion is structurally left to chance.

## Hypothesis
We believe an explicit, warm arrival state — "Someone sent you this garden — make it yours" with a single clear adoption path — will convert opened share links into `plan_created` at a measurable rate, because the recipient currently gets a generic report page with no context or call to action. Confidence: guess (no recipient traffic yet — this readies the funnel before 005 generates it).

## Change
Smallest version: when `sharedPlan` decodes non-null (planner.js ~3286), render ONE dismissible arrival banner at the top of the plan view with recipient-framed copy and a single CTA into their (already-prefilled) plan. FIRST investigate where shared plans actually land (trace `modeState` init ~3299-3320: report vs convo) and place the banner in the real path — add findings to Territory notes before coding. Verify `plan_created` actually fires for an adopting recipient via the existing persist effect (trace it; document the exact chain in notes); do NOT add new events and do NOT re-fire anything for the sender.

There is NO sender name in the URL — do not fabricate one ("Someone sent you…" is the honest framing).

OUT of scope: changing `encodePlanToUrl`/`decodePlanFromUrl` or any URL semantics, router changes, new analytics events, sender identity/referral params, styling beyond the banner.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Opening a URL produced by `encodePlanToUrl` in a fresh context shows the arrival framing; normal visits (no share params, presets, fresh=1, saved-plan returns) NEVER show it
- [ ] Banner is dismissible; dismissal respects `prefers-reduced-motion`; neumorphic tokens only; no banned effects; sensible at 360/768/1280
- [ ] The recipient's adopted plan fires `plan_created` (trace the persist effect end-to-end; documented in 007-notes.md with file:line)
- [ ] New strings in BOTH en and ko (add keys immediately AFTER the existing `checkoutNote` key in each planner block — NOT at the end — to avoid colliding with 005's edits)
- [ ] `node test_planner.js && node test_protocol_parsing.js && node test_qualifier_fix.js` exit 0

## Measurement
Metric: funnel `share_link_opened → plan_created` (the north star itself) · Window: 14 days (until 2026-07-24) · Decision rule: any closure > 0 validates the moment; refine copy/CTA from there.
