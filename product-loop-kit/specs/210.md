# Spec 210: pool-detail information architecture — the same numbers five times, and the CTA nowhere near the intent peak

## Evidence

Human UX audit of the live pool-detail page (2026-08-02, screenshot of `?pool=…` jupiter-lend USDC on
Solana, 5.19% APY, $421.4M TVL). Section map read from `PoolDetail.js` on `origin/main` @ `09f090c6c`:

| # | line | section |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `:519` | `pool-hero-card` — identity + chips + `pool-action-card` (`:657`: APY, 2 CTAs, 2 hints) |
| 2 | `:719` | `pool-projection-card` — "THE LONG GAME" |
| 3 | `:805` | daily-earnings card |
| 4 | `:840` | monthly-earnings card |
| 5 | `:836` | risk-assessment card |
| 6 | `:908` | `calculator-compact` (amount, presets, 1D/7D/30D, result) |
| 7 | `:1249` | Pool Information (6 tiles + 2 rate notes + underlying assets) |
| 8 | `:1807` | repeat `pool-action-card` — "Ready to start this garden?" |

**Measured duplication on one screen** (counted from the rendered page):

- `~$1,288 in 5y` — **3×** (hero CTA label, Long Game headline, bottom CTA label)
- `$4.32` monthly — **2×** (stat card 4, calculator result)
- `4.47% base / 0.72% rewards` — **2×** (hero APY breakdown `:672`, Pool Info BASE/REWARD APY tiles)
- `$1,000` basis restated — **4×** ("on $1,000" ×2, "$1,000 in this pool", "Based on $1,000 investment")
- `"Estimates based on current rates — yields change constantly. Not financial advice."` — **2×**
- The **entire CTA block** (2 buttons + "No wallet needed" + "Opens protocol • Wallet required") — **2×**, verbatim
- Of Pool Information's 6 tiles, **3 are duplicates**: BASE APY and REWARD APY repeat the hero; POOL TYPE
  repeats the `YIELD FARMING` chip.

**Structural findings:**

1. **Sections 2–6 are five presentations of one computation.** The Long Game, daily, monthly, and the
   calculator all render `investmentAmount × rate` over different periods. The three stat cards *are* the
   calculator's output at its default. The user scrolls through the same arithmetic four times before
   reaching anything new.
2. **The most demotivating framing is given the most prominence.** `Daily earnings $0.14` is the first
   number in the stat row. For a cautious-saver ICP thinking in life goals, $0.14/day is the least
   compelling true statement the page can make; the 5-year figure is the one that matches the archetype.
3. **The CTA is not at the intent peak.** The repeat CTA sits at page bottom (`:1807`) *after* Pool
   Information's reference tiles. The actual peak is the moment the user types their own number into the
   calculator — and at that moment there is no CTA on screen at all.
4. **The page's most differentiated content is buried.** The two rate-trust notes ("Steady so far: across
   the 20 days we've tracked it…" and the 1.09% climb note, `:1604`+) are the only content on the page that
   DefiLlama itself does not have — the loop spent items 087/088.1/103 building them — and they render
   below a collapsible fold, under six reference tiles.
5. **Two CTAs of near-equal weight pull in opposite directions.** "Garden this pool →" (stay) and "Start
   Earning on jupiter-lend ↗" (leave). Both count toward the north star, so neither may be removed — but
   presenting them as near-peers at both call sites splits the decision instead of guiding it.
6. **The hero wastes its strongest real estate.** The left ~60% is empty below the chips while the right
   column carries APY + 2 buttons + 2 hint lines. `Verified` and `$421.4M TVL` — the page's primary
   credibility signals — render as small low-contrast chips; `Low risk` is stranded in stat card 5, far from
   the APY it qualifies.

**Why it matters now:** north star `pool_click{garden_cta|protocol_link}` = **0 for 25 days** (`signals/
2026-08-02.md`), and this is the surface the only observed real arrival landed on.

**Re-based on `origin/main` @ `09f090c6c` — three facts that post-date the audit screenshot and BIND this
item.** Item 207 shipped between the audit and this spec and touched this exact file:

1. **207 added `rateHistoryUnavailable`** (`PoolDetail.js:1604`, `translations.js:50` EN / `:779` KO) — the
   honest "we don't have a rate history for this pool" note for sub-rail SEO arrivals. **This is a fourth
   member of the rate-note family and must survive the rearrangement**, following the same placement rule
   as the other notes. Do not re-collapse it into the blank it replaced.
2. **There is a THIRD `trackPoolClick` source on this page**, not two: `defillama_fallback`
   (`PoolDetail.js:262`), alongside `protocol_link` (`:235`) and `garden_cta` (`:690`, `:1826`). It is not
   part of the north-star filter, but it is a live emitter on a CTA being moved — **preserve it exactly**.
3. **Every CTA now emits a `ctaPlacement` property.** This is load-bearing for this item: it is how the
   rearrangement becomes measurable. When the repeat CTA moves into the earnings block, its `ctaPlacement`
   value must change to name its NEW position (not silently keep the old string), so hero-vs-earnings-block
   click share is readable from the existing event without any new instrumentation.

The `:1807` repeat-CTA block also now carries a comment stating its rationale — *"can convert without
scrolling back up"*. That intent is correct and this item does not discard it; it relocates the block to
where the intent actually peaks rather than deleting the affordance.

## Hypothesis

We believe **collapsing the five redundant presentations into one interactive block, moving the repeat CTA
to the moment the user sets their own amount, and promoting credibility + rate-trust content next to the
numbers they qualify** will **increase `pool_click{garden_cta|protocol_link}`**, because the current page
makes the user scroll past the same arithmetic four times to reach reference data, and offers no CTA at the
point of highest intent. Confidence: **supported** (the duplication is objectively counted, not judged; the
IA principle — credibility → headline number → your number → act → reference — is standard) — **not
validated**, and at current traffic it cannot be validated by metric (see Measurement).

## Change

**Keep every existing component and CSS class.** This is a re-ordering and de-duplication, not a redesign.
No new component, no new visual language, no gradient/off-brand one-offs (NORTH_STAR 2026-07-10).

**New order — credibility → the number → your number → act → reference detail:**

**1. Hero (`:519`) — consolidate credibility, one clear CTA**
- Move the **risk level** (`riskAssessment.level` + `.description`, currently the `:836` card) into the
  hero's existing chip row alongside `✓ Verified` and `$421.4M TVL`. Reuse the chip classes; do not invent a
  new badge. Delete the standalone `:836` card once moved.
- Move **one** rate-trust note (the steadiness note, `rateTrackRecord*` family) to a single line directly
  under the APY breakdown in `pool-action-card`. It qualifies the 5.19%; it belongs next to it.
- Keep `Garden this pool →` as the sole visually-primary CTA. **Demote** `Start Earning on <protocol> ↗` to
  the existing quieter link treatment (`pool-action-hint--muted` sibling styling) — still present, still
  above the fold, still firing its event.
- **Shorten the primary CTA label to `Garden this pool →`.** The `~$1,288 in 5y` projection moves to the
  earnings block where the user controls the input; a button should name the action, not carry a forecast
  the user has not yet parameterised.

**2. Merge sections 2, 3, 4 and 6 into ONE "your garden" block** (reuse `calculator-compact` as the shell,
absorbing `pool-projection-card`'s copy):
- **Input first**: amount field + preset chips at the top of the block, so the user sets their number before
  reading a result.
- **Headline**: the Long Game sentence, recomputed live from the input — *"$1,000 in this pool grows to
  ~$1,288 in 5y at current rates."*
- **Frame line, kept verbatim**: *"Your deposit stays yours — you keep your money, and it keeps working."*
  (the v3 yield-funded flip — this is the product's pitch, do not cut it).
- **Secondary readout**: the 1D/7D/30D toggle feeding **one** figure. **Remove the standalone daily and
  monthly stat cards** (`:805`, `:840`) — the toggle already produces both, and `$0.14/day` must not be the
  page's most prominent number.
- **ONE disclaimer** in this block. Delete the duplicate.
- **Move the repeat CTA block here**, at the end of this block — the intent peak. Reuse the `:1807`
  `pool-action-card` markup as-is.

**3. Pool Information (`:1249`) — de-duplicate and re-rank**
- **Remove** the `BASE APY`, `REWARD APY` and `POOL TYPE` tiles (all three duplicate the hero).
- **Keep** `30D MEAN APY`, `EXPOSURE`, `IL RISK`; **add** `TVL` so the tile grid stays balanced.
- **Promote the remaining rate-trust note(s) above the tiles** — prose insight outranks reference data.
- Underlying Assets unchanged.

**4. Delete the page-bottom CTA block (`:1807`)** — it has moved to step 2. The page should not end on a
verbatim repeat.

**OUT of scope, explicitly:** any trust-rail change (`APY_SANITY_LIMIT`, `DEFAULT_MIN_TVL`, anomaly
flags/demotion, degen ⅓ haircut); any change to which pools render; any change to the two CTAs' hrefs,
event names or event properties; any new dependency; `app.js`, `planner.js`, `home.html`, `canonical.js`,
generators, `data/`. New copy is limited to re-labelling moved elements — no new marketing claims.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] **All three click emitters still fire, unchanged.** `PoolDetail.js` still calls
      `Analytics.trackPoolClick` with `source` values `garden_cta`, `protocol_link` **and**
      `defillama_fallback`, exactly once each per click, with byte-identical event name and `source`
      strings. Verified by rendered Playwright click on each CTA on **both** entry paths (url_direct and
      card_click), asserting the emitted payloads — this is the north star and a silent regression here is
      the worst outcome of the item.
- [ ] **`ctaPlacement` distinguishes the moved CTA from the hero CTA.** The relocated repeat CTA emits a
      placement value naming its new position, distinct from the hero's, so click share by placement is
      readable from the existing event. A moved CTA that keeps the old placement string is a FAIL — it
      would make the item unmeasurable even once traffic exists.
- [ ] **207's `rateHistoryUnavailable` note still renders** for a pool with no `kpis`, in EN and KO, and is
      not orphaned or duplicated by the rearrangement. Assert on a rendered sub-rail pool.
- [ ] Both CTAs are present and reachable above the fold at 1280px, and the primary is visually dominant.
- [ ] **De-duplication is asserted, not eyeballed:** on a rendered page, the projection string
      (`~$X in 5y`) appears **at most twice** (headline + one CTA context) where it appeared 3×; the
      disclaimer sentence appears **exactly once**; `BASE APY`/`REWARD APY`/`POOL TYPE` tiles are absent from
      Pool Information; the standalone daily and monthly stat cards are absent.
- [ ] The repeat CTA renders **inside** the earnings block, and no CTA block renders after Pool Information.
- [ ] Risk level renders in the hero chip row; the standalone `:836` risk card is gone.
- [ ] At least one rate-trust note renders in the hero next to the APY; the remaining note(s) render
      **above** Pool Information's tiles.
- [ ] **Trust rails byte-untouched.** `APY_SANITY_LIMIT`, `DEFAULT_MIN_TVL`, the degen ⅓ haircut and the
      anomaly-flag logic are unmodified; the degen-haircut warning (`:765`) and anomalous-pool warning
      (`:768`) still render, still visible, not relocated below a fold. **An anomalous pool must still be

      `toLocaleString()` introduced.
- [ ] Responsive + dark: no horizontal body scroll at **360 / 768 / 1280**, dark mode renders, focus rings
      present. **Per item 136's trap, assert the primary CTA's bounding box is fully inside the viewport at
      360px** — a `scrollWidth`-only check passes on a page whose main CTA is clipped by an
      `overflow:hidden` ancestor.
- [ ] `prefers-reduced-motion` respected for any animation touched.
- [ ] No new CSS class and no new component — verifier greps the diff for added class names and for
      gradient/off-brand values.

- [ ] Rendered-Playwright on the real UI, not unit fixtures (NORTH_STAR standing decision 2026-07-11).
- [ ] Instrumentation: **no new event.** `pool_view` and `pool_click{source}` already measure this surface

## Measurement

Metric: `pool_click{source ∈ garden_cta, protocol_link}` and CTR = that ÷ `pool_view` · Window: 14 days ·
Decision rule: **cannot be validated by metric at current traffic** — 30d `pool_view` = 6, `pool_click` = 0,
far below the ≥30 minimum. So: **keep** if every rendered acceptance criterion holds and no guardrail moves
(`error_occurred` stays 0, planner conversion unchanged); record **INCONCLUSIVE at n≈0** in `LEARNINGS.md`
at window close, the honest disposition 088.1 took on 2026-07-28. **Do not report a conversion effect in
either direction.** If traffic clears ≥30 `pool_view` inside the window, compare CTR pre/post and write the
real number.

## Risk tier (builder's guess — verifier assigns independently)

**HIGH.** User-facing well beyond copy/styling, on the north-star surface, touching the render path of both
north-star CTAs and of the anomaly/haircut warnings that are trust rails. The specific failure mode to hunt
is a **silently dropped or double-fired `trackPoolClick`** during the CTA move, which would zero the north
star while every visual check passes.

## Open questions

None blocking. One judgment for the builder, to be argued in `209-pr.md`: whether the 1D/7D/30D toggle keeps
a daily option at all. Recommendation: keep the toggle intact (removing user control is not the goal) but
ensure the **default** is 30D so `$0.14` is never the first figure rendered.
