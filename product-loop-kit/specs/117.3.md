## Hypothesis
We believe biasing the planner's auto-pick toward steadier-yield pools **only among near-APY-equal rail-passing candidates** will make plans lean on more stable rates (the quantified form of 088's "steady 6% beats spiky 20%") **without lowering the honest headline blended rate materially or overstating it**, because the change is a tie-break, not a re-rank — it can only reorder pools whose APYs are already effectively equal. Confidence: validated-pattern (same "land now, activates ~07-22 as data accrues" mechanism 117.1/117.2 shipped; the direction — prefer steadier — is the product's stated trust thesis).

## Change
In `planner.js` `curatePools` (the `eligible.sort(...)` block, ~L606-623 — all three branches: `preferTypes`, `rwaAllowlist`, and the plain `else`), add a **Sharpe near-tie-break** as the LAST comparator key, applied only after the existing primary keys (preferType/rwa membership, then `poolTotalApy` DESC):
- When two eligible pools' `poolTotalApy` are within a small epsilon of each other (near-tie — e.g. `abs(apyA − apyB) <= max(0.10, 0.02 * max(apyA, apyB))`, i.e. within 0.1pp or 2% of the larger rate, whichever is greater; builder tunes the exact epsilon and documents it), prefer the pool with the higher numeric `kpis.apySharpe`.
- A pool with a materially higher `poolTotalApy` (outside the epsilon) is NEVER demoted below a lower-APY pool because of Sharpe — the primary APY order wins outside near-ties.
- Pools with null/undefined/non-numeric `apySharpe` keep their existing APY-order position (treated as neutral — never sorted below a same-APY numeric-Sharpe pool via a crash or NaN; a numeric Sharpe wins a near-tie against a null, null-vs-null keeps prior order).

Explicitly OUT of scope:
- The rail-filter block (`curatePools` L594-604): `apy<=0`, `apy>band.maxApy`, `tvlUsd<band.minTvl`, `stableOnly`, exclude/chain/token opts — byte-untouched.
- Trust rails: `APY_SANITY_LIMIT`, `DEFAULT_MIN_TVL`, anomaly demotion, degen ⅓ haircut (`effectiveApy`), `blendedApy`/`median` math — byte-untouched.
- No new user-facing copy in the smallest version (silent ordering preference) → `translations.js` untouched. (If the builder judges a micro-note genuinely necessary, it ships EN + natural KO together — but the default is no new string.)
- 117.1 (pool-detail) / 117.2 (analytics sort) surfaces — already shipped, untouched.
- `home.html` `__APP_MODE` router, `?token=/?chain=/?pool=` behavior — not on this path.
