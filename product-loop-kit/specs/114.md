# 114 — "Welcome back" saved-garden re-entry card on the search-first landing

**Status:** READY · **Score:** 7.2 · **Risk:** HIGH (touches the search-first landing surface, adjacent to the IA router) · **Theme:** hook-model retention → card funnel

## Why (evidence — browser-driven discovery 2026-07-15)
Drove the real UI in Chromium against the local snapshot (`scratchpad/shots/`, `reports/2026-07-15-hook-model-discovery.md`):
- The planner's return-visit **report mode** is genuinely built: reloading `/plan.html` with a saved `garden-plan` renders "🌱 Your garden since Jul 15, 2026 · Spotify · $2,900 capital · Safe & Steady — **Still on track, your rates are holding steady**", garden stage (Planted→Growing), forever-number, and a "Next: Amazon Prime at ≈$6,500" ladder nudge. This is the hook model's **variable reward** surface. (`planner.js:3872` `useState(savedPlan ? 'report' : 'convo')`; report render ~3320–3390; `reportStats` :378; `gardenStage` :3241.)
- **But the actual default entry point is now the search-first landing at bare `/`** (item #237, `landing.js`) — not `/plan.html`. Browser run: with a saved `garden-plan` in localStorage, loading `/` shows **no trace of the saved garden** (`landingReturn.showsSavedGarden: false`) — just the cold "Find yield with a little more clarity" search hero. The `investment → variable-reward` bridge exists only on a page returning users no longer land on by default.

Net: a user who plants a garden and comes back to `defi.garden` (bookmark, repeat visit) lands cold. The hook loop's payoff is one click away but never surfaced. Reconnecting it at the real front door is the cheapest retention win available.

## What to build
In **`landing.js`**, when a valid saved plan exists in `localStorage['garden-plan']`, render a neumorphic **re-entry card** in the hero area — reuse/augment the existing `.landing-garden-card` aside (the "Have a goal in mind? / Plant a garden" card), OR render a sibling card above it. When a saved plan exists, this card takes priority over the generic "Plant a garden" prompt (the generic prompt still shows for first-time visitors with no saved plan).

Card content (all via `translations.js`, EN + natural KO together):
- Leaf mark + "Welcome back" caption (new key, e.g. `landing.returnCaption`).
- The garden's goal label + one-line status. **Reuse existing plan fields** — read `garden-plan`'s `goal`/`mix`/`archetype`/`hero`/`savedAt`; derive the goal label the same way the planner does. Show the garden-stage emoji from a **read-only** reuse of the stage logic (do NOT duplicate `gardenStage` — either import the pure helper if the module boundary allows, or show a static "🌱 your garden" line; prefer not re-implementing rate math on the landing).
- A one-tap primary link to `plan.html` (report mode auto-loads there because a saved plan exists — no new param needed). Label e.g. `landing.returnCta` = "Tend your garden →" (KO: "정원 돌보기 →"). Reuse the existing `ArrowIcon`.
- Keep it honest and calm: no fake progress, no urgency. If the saved plan is malformed/old-schema, fail safe to the existing generic card (wrap the parse in try/catch, exactly like `landing.js`'s existing theme/lang localStorage reads).

Styling: **neuro tokens only** (`--neuro-shadow-raised/-subtle`, `--neuro-radius-md`), light source top-left, `:active` sink. No gradients, no scale-pop. Must be flawless at 360/768/1280 and in dark mode; `prefers-reduced-motion` respected (reuse the existing `.landing-reveal` reveal, or none).
