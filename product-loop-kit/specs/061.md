# Spec 061: Waitlist reframe to the card value prop

## Evidence
Human directive, NORTH_STAR.md 2026-07-12 interview Q2 (verbatim): "the yield-funded subscription CARD. Users deposit with us into the protocol (position always self-manageable), each position gets a DISPOSABLE CARD that pays a subscription from the yield (e.g. $23/mo yield → Claude sub); we pocket card fees. The waitlist is the card's funnel... Card copy stays honest: it's a waitlist for early access, the card does not exist yet — no fake availability." North-star metric switched the same day from viral-loop closure to weekly `waitlist_submitted` count.

Audit of the current waitlist flow (`planner.js:2588-2599` live CTA → `2430-2507` modal, copy in `translations.js:461-496`/`1015-1051`) shows the copy never mentions a card, a deposit, or "yield pays the bill" as the mechanic — it reads as a generic email waitlist ("Join the early access list — we'll email you when your spot is ready. No wallet or crypto experience needed to sign up."). The one place that gestures at the mechanic, `waitlistGarden`, is a conditional aside ("...≈$X/mo paid from yield forever") shown only when the visitor has picked bundle items, and never uses the word "card." The CTA that opens the modal is the checkout panel's `.gp-primary-cta.gp-checkout-cta` (rendered in both subscription and growth/target layouts); a second, identically-labelled CTA (`ctaElement`, `planner.js:2348-2364`) is dead code — never inserted into either returned tree (confirmed in `specs/009-notes.md`) — and needs no separate edit since it shares the same translation keys.

## Hypothesis
We believe naming the actual product (a disposable card, funded by the visitor's own yield, that pays a subscription automatically) in the waitlist CTA and modal — instead of generic "early access list" language — will lift `waitlist_opened → waitlist_submitted` conversion, because visitors currently have no way to know what they're signing up for beyond "an email later." Confidence: guess (no prior card-specific waitlist copy has been tested).

## Change
`translations.js` only (EN + KO, both blocks, same keys, updated together):
1. `ctaWaitlistMicro` (461-463 EN / 1016-1017 KO) — replace the generic third fact with an explicit "card doesn't exist yet" honesty note (no-fake-availability, per NORTH_STAR).
   - EN: `"Free to join • Card doesn't exist yet • We'll email you when it does"`
   - KO: `"무료 가입 • 카드는 아직 없어요 • 준비되면 이메일로 알려드려요"`
2. `waitlistTitle` (466 / 1020) — from generic "spot" framing to the card itself.
   - EN: `"Get early access to the card"`
   - KO: `"카드 얼리 액세스 신청하기"`
3. `waitlistBenefits` (467 / 1021) — state the yield-pays-subscription-via-a-card mechanic in plain terms, keep the "we'll email you" honest-early-access framing. **Constraint discovered mid-build**: `test_planner.js`'s pre-existing "waitlist copy — ICP alignment" suite (~line 1054) bans "self-custody", "hold the keys", and the exact phrase "disposable cards" (EN) / "일회용 카드", "셀프 커스터디" (KO) — the ICP is jargon-averse, so "card" is fine but "disposable card" is not, at this stage. Copy revised to comply.
   - EN: `"Join early access — once it's ready, your garden's yield pays your subscription automatically through a simple card. Your money always stays yours; no wallet or crypto experience needed to sign up."`
   - KO: `"얼리 액세스에 가입하는 거예요 — 준비되면 내 정원의 수익으로 구독료가 자동 결제되는 카드를 받게 돼요. 예치금은 항상 내 소유이고, 가입에 지갑이나 크립토 경험은 필요 없어요."`
4. `waitlistGarden` (468 / 1022, the personalized conditional line) — tie the number explicitly to "a card," not just "paid from yield."
   - EN: `` (labels, monthly) => `Your garden already covers ${labels} — a card funded by ≈${monthly}/mo of yield could pay it forever.` ``
   - KO: `` (labels, monthly) => `내 정원은 이미 ${labels}를 커버해요 — 월 ≈${monthly}의 수익으로 결제되는 카드가 영구히 대신 내줄 수 있어요.` ``

OUT of scope: `ctaWaitlist` button label (kept as "Join the waitlist →" / existing KO — "waitlist" is the correct honest word per NORTH_STAR and changing it risks losing the established, already-tested CTA), step-2 accepted screen (`waitlistAccepted`/`waitlistNextSteps`/`waitlistJumpLine` — post-signup, not the value-prop moment), the dead `ctaElement` render-tree bug (pre-existing, flagged in 009-notes, not this item's job to fix since it shares the same keys and the live CTA already works), `doWaitlistShare`/`doWaitlistDownload` (024 already scoped these out), Formspree payload/instrumentation (009 already shipped and unchanged here), any component/CSS/layout change.
