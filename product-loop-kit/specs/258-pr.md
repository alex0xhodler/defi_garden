# 258 — PR explainer (LOW tier)

**Goal.** Make a pool-card click say *where it was rendered*, in one property.

**Why it mattered.** The only prod `pool_click` in 33 days (`yield_calculator`, 2026-08-09) took a
three-property forensic join to attribute — `$current_url` said `?token=USDC``, the pool was
`USDT/aave-v3`, and `app.js:840`'s `includes()` matcher proves a USDT pool cannot be a search result on
that page, so the click had to have come from the item-012/072 rescue grid. `source` names the **widget**
(`garden_cta` / `protocol_link` / `yield_calculator`); nothing named the **surface**. The information
existed at render time and was thrown away.

**What changed.**

1. `app.js` — `renderPoolCard` takes a `surface`, threaded into `handlePoolClick` (fires `pool_view`) and
   `handleCalculateYield` (fires both events; its `trackPoolClick` call previously passed no context object
   at all). The three call sites pass `'results'`, `'empty_state_alternatives'`, `'dead_pool_alternatives'`.
2. `analytics.js` — `trackPoolView`/`trackPoolClick` forward the key **explicitly and guarded**
   (`if (context.surface !== undefined) …`), never a `...context` spread. Emitters that pass no surface
   produce a payload with **no `surface` key** — absence, not `null`, so pre-258 events are unchanged.
3. `test_pool_click_surface.js` — LEG A parses `app.js` at test time so a future `renderPoolCard` call
   site written as a **direct call** (the existing convention) without a surface goes RED, with no
   browser; LEG B drives all three grids in a real browser and spies at the `Analytics.track`
   choke-point (not the helper's arguments — the 214 defect lived in that gap). **Bound on LEG A,
   established by the verifier's own experiment, not assumed:** the scan is *lexical* — it finds calls
   by the literal substring `renderPoolCard(`. Route a call through an alias, `.call`/`.apply`/`.bind`,
   or any indirection and LEG A does not see it; it reports a population of 2 instead of 3 and does
   **not** flag the shrink. For the three grids that exist today that gap is closed by LEG B, whose
   payload-shape assertion requires `surface` unconditionally on every context it drives — but a
   *genuinely new* grid, reached indirectly and not among LEG B's three URLs, would be caught by
   neither leg. Closing that needs an AST parse or a shrinking-population assertion; neither is built,
   and this sentence is the disclosure rather than a ticket.
4. `app.compiled.js` / `app.compiled.min.js` regenerated; the new test registered in `test:serial`.

**Non-vacuity.** Both mutations executed and recorded in `258-notes.md`: strip the `analytics.js`
forwarding → 3 assertions RED; strip one call site's argument → the population check RED; both restored
byte-identical by md5 → 8/8 GREEN.

**What this does NOT claim.** Nothing here says the rescue empty state converts — the click that motivated
it was crawler-classified. The traffic-gated question opens at ≥30 **real** `pool_view` carrying
`surface: 'empty_state_alternatives'`; today that count is 0. And the class is not closed: 2 of the repo's
32 emittable events now carry a render-context property; the other 30 do not, and nothing guards them.

**Risk: LOW.** Analytics instrumentation, additive property, guarded forwarding. No trust rail, no IA
router, no parameterized-URL behavior, no generated SEO surface, no new dependency, no user-facing string
(so no translations.js work required).
