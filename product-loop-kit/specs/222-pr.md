# 222 — PR explainer (HIGH tier)

**What shipped:** on the analytics results surface (`?token=` / `?chain=` — the pages
every SEO lander resolves to), the theme and language controls now work on mobile. Before
this, four such buttons rendered and **not one of them could be pressed** at 360 or 480px.

Risk tier **HIGH**, independently assigned by the verifier and matching the spec's
proposal: `NORTH_STAR.md`'s risk policy names parameterized-URL render behaviour
explicitly ("`?token=/?chain=/?pool=` are sacred sitemap URLs"). **Not** on the NEVER list
— no trust rail, no credentials/money, no SEO surface deleted, no out-of-scope directory,
no JS touched.

---

## 1. The goal, in one line

A cautious retail saver landing on `defi.garden/?token=USDC` from search, on a phone,
could not switch the site to dark mode or to Korean. Not "it was awkward" — the controls
were physically unreachable. This makes them reachable, and proves it with a rendered
gate so it cannot silently come back.

## 2. The intuition — why FOUR buttons and ZERO working ones

The app renders its theme/language controls in two places:

- **In the sticky header** (`app.js:3057/3062`), shown only on results pages.
- **As a standalone floating pair** (`app.js:3143/3153`), rendered unconditionally as
  direct children of `.app` — these are the *only* controls on the analytics homepage,
  which renders no header at all.

On a results page both sets exist at once. Three independent CSS faults then conspired:

1. **The floating rules leaked.** `.theme-toggle { position: fixed; … }` (`style.css:3913`)
   and its `.language-toggle` twin (`style.css:4029`) are unscoped. The header's own
   buttons carry `className: 'google-control-btn theme-toggle'` — *both* classes — so those
   rules matched them too and pinned them to the standalone pair's coordinates. At 768px
   the header's language button and the standalone one occupied the byte-identical rect
   `x:500 y:20 w:48 h:48`.

2. **The header row could not shrink.** `.google-header-content` (`style.css:928`) is a
   flex row with **no `@media` override anywhere in the file**, and its `flex: 1` search
   container and input both lacked `min-width: 0` — so neither could shrink below the
   input's intrinsic ~170px. At 360px the trailing controls were pushed to `x:498.8`,
   past the edge, with `documentElement.scrollWidth` still 360: **clipped, not
   scrollable-to**.

3. **The standalone pair was buried.** At ≤640px `.theme-toggle` becomes
   `position: static` (`style.css:4319`), and the only rule restoring `fixed` is
   `.app:not(.has-results) .theme-toggle` — which by construction never matches a results
   page. So they rendered in flow, under the 130px fixed header, hit-testing to
   `.google-search-input` and `.google-nav-tab`.

Measured at 360×780, at rest, EN and KO identical:

| element | rect | `elementFromPoint` at centre | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| header `theme-toggle` | `x:562.8 y:31` | `null` | off-screen, clipped |
| header `language-toggle` | `x:498.8 y:24` | `null` | off-screen, clipped |
| standalone `theme-toggle` | `x:284 y:32` | `input.google-search-input` | buried |
| standalone `language-toggle` | `x:0 y:70` | `button.google-nav-tab.active` | buried |

## 3. What changed, in reading order

**`style.css`** — four blocks, styling only, each with a house-style comment naming the
item and the measurement it is sized from. No new class, colour, shadow, radius, gradient
or token; no JS; `app.js` has zero diff.

1. `.google-header-controls .theme-toggle, … .language-toggle { position: static; top:
   auto; right: auto; margin: 0; z-index: auto; }` — puts the header's own buttons back in
   the header's flow. Chosen over `:not(.google-control-btn)` scoping because that would
   mean editing ~6 declarations across three media tiers; this two-class selector
   (specificity 0,2,0) beats every one-class floating rule (0,1,0) in every tier, media
   queries adding no specificity of their own.
2. `@media (max-width: 640px)` — `min-width: 0` on `.google-search-container` and
   `.google-search-input`, plus a tighter `gap`/`padding` on `.google-header-content`, so
   the row can actually shrink and the controls stay on-screen.
3. `.app.has-results > .theme-toggle, … > .language-toggle { display: none; }` — hides the
   now-redundant standalone duplicates on results pages **only**. The `>` child combinator
   is load-bearing: the header's buttons are grandchildren of `.app`, so a descendant
   selector would have hidden them too. Scoped to `.has-results`, so the analytics
   homepage — which has no header to fall back on — keeps its working pair.
4. `@media (max-width: 768px) { .app.has-results { padding-top: 140px } }` — raised from
   `var(--space-20)` (20px). **This is the whole story of attempt 2; see §4.**

**`style.min.css`** — regenerated via `npm run minify`. `home.html:134` loads the minified
sheet, so skipping this ships nothing (the item 136/061 trap); the new selectors and the
`padding-top:140px` value were grepped out of the compiled file rather than assumed.

**`test_mobile_controls_reachable.js`** (new, 628 lines) — the rendered gate. Eleven
criteria across 360/480/640/768/1280 × EN/KO × light/dark × `?token=` and `?chain=`,
fixture-routed against the real minified sheets, always at `scrollY === 0` (asserted,
never scrolled). Criterion 2 is a **hit test**, not rect algebra — a rect can miss
intersection by sub-pixel rounding while the overlay still steals the tap, and the tap is
what the user cares about. Criterion 4 uses a real Playwright `click()`, which throws on
interception. Criterion 8 guards block 3's scoping by asserting the homepage's standalone
pair still works. Three red proofs prove the gate can go red.

**`package.json`** — one line appended to `test:serial` (from which `run-tests.js` derives
its registry; `test_test_registry.js` enforces no orphans).

## 4. The deviation that matters: attempt 1 failed verification

Attempt 1 shipped blocks 1-3, passed **17/17** of its own rendered criteria, and was
**wrong**. The verifier found — and the operator then reproduced against a true
`git checkout f66ea3fde5 -- style.css style.min.css` baseline — that hiding the standalone
pair had introduced a new P1 occlusion on this item's own target surface:

| width | baseline `f66ea3fde5` | attempt 1 |
|---|---|---|
| 360 | `.results-title` `y:141.7`, header h130 → clear | `y:49.7`, header h120 → **occluded**, hits `.google-header-content` |
| 480 | clear | **occluded** |
| 640 | `y:169.7`, header h140 → clear | `y:57.7` → **occluded** |
| 768 | `y:57.7` → already occluded (PRE-EXISTING) | unchanged |
| 1280 | clear | clear |

`.app.has-results`'s mobile clearance was only 20px against a 120-127px fixed header —
arithmetic that should have buried the heading all along. It didn't, because the two
**broken** standalone buttons were sitting in flow above the content contributing ~92px of
margin. The clearance keeping the page readable was **accidental, and supplied by the very
defect being fixed**. `.container`'s top went from `y:112` to `y:20` the moment they were
hidden.

The original spec's Territory note said not to touch that `padding-top`, on the grounds
that the grid below measured clear. That premise was only true because of the toggles, so
the note was explicitly superseded in the attempt-2 addendum rather than quietly ignored.

Block 4 replaces the accidental clearance with a declared one. `140px` is sized from
measurement — header heights of 120 (EN)/123 (KO) at ≤640px and 124/127 at 700-768px,
max **127**, with `.results-title` ~29.7px below `.container`'s top → ~169.7px, clearing
by ~42px. The addendum pre-registered a ≥135px floor and a ~160px stop-and-report ceiling
so the number could not be fitted to whatever turned the suite green. Verified at 360, 390,
414, 480, 640, 700 and 768px in **both** languages.

It also clears the **768px case that was broken on baseline too** — disclosed rather than
carved out, because the same single value fixes both.

## 5. What was deliberately NOT done

- **`app.js` untouched.** Deleting the standalone buttons would have been a smaller CSS
  diff and would have broken the analytics homepage, which has no header. Criterion 8 is
  the standing guard.
- **`test_audit_app.js`'s `QUARANTINED_OCCLUSION_SURFACES` left alone.** The run prints
  `QUARANTINE NO LONGER NEEDED for [grid-chain]` (and, on the verifier's run, for
  `grid-token`) — that is the documented 1-of-5 boundary flake from `specs/219-notes.md`
  "Round 3, R3.4", not a clearance. Removing an entry would have made the gate
  intermittently red.
- **The remaining `grid-360` footer-band occlusion** (`.pool-symbol`/`.tvl-label` under
  `.app-footer` at rest) is item 219's separate `(b)` class and is untouched — its own
  ticket, not folded in here.
- **The full `test_serial` (~140 files)** was not run: the 5-minute foreground timebox
  (standing decision 2026-07-11). Eleven rails that could plausibly be affected were run
  individually instead, twice each — once by the builder and once independently by both
  the operator and the verifier.
- **`test_search.js`** needs ~540s in this sandbox, over the timebox. Not run. Stated, not
  glossed.

## 6. How to check it by eye

Open `https://www.defi.garden/?token=USDC` on a phone (or Chrome DevTools at 360×780).
There should be exactly one theme toggle and one language toggle, both inside the header
row, both tappable, and the "Yields for USDC" heading should sit clearly below the header
rather than behind it. Repeat with `?lang=ko` and in dark mode. **This needs a human
eyeball**: it is a render-path change on a sacred parameterized surface, and the grid now
starts ~28px lower on mobile than it did before.

None, stated plainly rather than invented. At ~85 sessions/14d the ≥30-events rule makes
