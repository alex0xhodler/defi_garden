## The problem being studied
When a user lands on `?token=X` (or `/tokens/<slug>`) for a token whose pools are ALL below the $10M TVL floor (small / new / low-LP), the app dead-ends: the ≥$10M static snapshot resolves instantly to empty and (per item 133) may never fetch the real sub-floor pools from live DefiLlama — or does so on the slow live path. Both of our FAST sources are $10M-floored:
- the static snapshot (`isRailedIn` ≥ $10M), and
- the D1 poller (109 floors at write time).
So obscure-token discovery is forced onto the slow live DefiLlama fetch (133's data need).

## The decision this study must make
Recommend ONE of:
- **(a)** Add a LOWER-floored *discovery* dataset served for token-specific queries — either lower the poller/snapshot floor for a discovery slice, or promote item 112's already-computed $1000-floored CI transient into a served artifact.
- **(b)** Keep the live DefiLlama fallback for below-floor discovery (simpler, slower).
