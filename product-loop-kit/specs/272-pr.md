# 272 — loop efficiency reset (human-directed, 2026-08-11)

## Why
Post-v2 the loop got more careful and less productive: avg attempts 0.97 → 1.72, 3-attempt runs 1.8% → 28%, one day of 100% process/parked spend, 6 open PRs with 4 duplicates or parks. The checks are not the problem — they found 4 real bugs this week. The problem is that a finding's cost, an artifact's length, and process work's share of the queue were all unbounded.

## What changed (prose only — 5 kit files, no scripts, no product code)
1. **Verdict semantics** (`agents/verifier.md`). Findings are typed by how they resolve. **BLOCKING** = only a code-behavior change fixes it → consumes an attempt. **AMEND** = a claim corrected or residue disclosed with a number → fixed in the same round, ships, consumes nothing. Oscillation: a BLOCKING finding introduced by the previous round's fix → park immediately, remaining budget void. Calibration the semantics must satisfy: 228 ("FAIL ×3, all documentation") ships round 1; 263's round-3 behavioral finding still parks.
2. **Ceremony caps** (`verifier.md` check 6 + `build.md` §0, identical tables). spec 80/150 · notes 60/200 · PR 40/100+quiz · LOG 300 chars · non-vacuity 1 per gate file / per rule · verifier rounds 1/2 · test:code 2:1/3:1. Over-cap = FAIL category `ceremony`, resolved by **deleting text** — never by another attempt.
3. **Pickup arithmetic** (`build.md` §1, mirrored as a filing cap in `heartbeat.md` §4). A process item is picked only when it beats the best READY product item by **>1.0**; max ONE in flight. Backtest: 263-process (8.0) beat 265-product (7.8) by 0.2 and that day shipped zero product impact — under this rule 265 wins.
4. **Wall clock** (`build.md` §0). T+10 dispatched · T+75 builder deadline (over → descope) · T+95 verifier round done · T+120 merged or parked. Terminal states: MERGED / PARKED-with-recipe / SKIPPED (<20k tokens). Nothing else.
5. **Impact + vitals** (`_template.md`, `build.md`, `heartbeat.md` §2d). Every spec carries `Impact: <north-star leg | guardrail | defect-count n→0 on <surface>>`, repeated verbatim as the PR body's first line; can't fill it → it's a process item by definition. Every heartbeat prints `vitals | ships/wk | tokens/ship | attempts | open PRs (oldest) | product:process`, with breach thresholds (parked PRs >2, process share >25%, tokens/ship >600k) filing at most ONE triage item.

`NORTH_STAR.md` records all five as a standing decision in the human's own framing.

## What did NOT change
The extension attack, criteria/new-check review, instrumentation, scope and deviation checks — untouched, standard unchanged. The reset re-scopes their consequences, not their teeth.
