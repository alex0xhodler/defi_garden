# Spec 050: hreflang + Korean static token/chain pages

## Evidence
Direct grep, 2026-07-12: `grep -c hreflang tokens/1inch.html` → **0** (story pages have 3: en/ko/x-default). All 2,108 static token/chain pages are English-only with no `hreflang` annotations, even though `ko` is a first-class app language (`translations.js` ships full en/ko; `?lang=ko` + localStorage `defi-garden-lang`; the planner and analytics app are fully localized).
- 2026 SOTA: `hreflang` on already-translatable content is a low-risk international-SEO reach expansion; Korean DeFi search is a real, underserved surface for this content. The app already has the translation infrastructure; the static SEO surface just doesn't use it.

## Hypothesis
We believe generating Korean versions of the token/chain pages (`/tokens/<slug>?lang=ko` or `/ko/tokens/<slug>` — URL scheme TBD in build) with proper reciprocal `hreflang` will open an untapped organic surface (Korean DeFi queries) at low marginal cost, because the translation layer already exists and the pages are template-generated. Confidence: **supported for the hreflang mechanics; the traffic upside is a genuine bet (no Korean-query baseline yet).**

## Change
This is the largest of the SEO items (~doubles the generated page count) — scope carefully:
1. Decide the i18n URL scheme (SOTA options: subdirectory `/ko/tokens/<slug>` — cleanest for hreflang and preferred by Google for programmatic i18n; vs. `?lang=ko` param — matches the app's existing convention but weaker as a distinct indexable URL). Default recommendation: **subdirectory** for the static pages, with reciprocal `hreflang` (en ↔ ko ↔ x-default), each self-canonical to its own language URL. Confirm with the human if it changes the vercel.json routing surface (that's HIGH/router-adjacent).
2. Token/chain generators: emit a Korean variant per page, with all user-facing strings (title, description, intro, answer block, FAQ, notes) routed through the existing `translations.js` `ko` catalog (extend the catalog with the token/chain-page keys — EN + natural KO together per CLAUDE.md's hard rule). Numbers stay en-US-formatted per CLAUDE.md.
3. Add reciprocal `hreflang` link tags to BOTH language variants; add the ko URLs to a sitemap (or extend the existing token/chain sitemaps with `xhtml:link` alternates).
4. Trust rails unchanged — the ko page shows the exact same gated pools as its en twin (translation is copy-only; every number derives from the same data through the same sanity rails).

OUT of scope: translating the app shell further (already done); machine-translating without the `translations.js` catalog (CLAUDE.md forbids one-language-only or ad-hoc translation); other languages beyond ko.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Each generated token/chain page has a Korean counterpart at the chosen URL scheme, with all user-facing copy from the `ko` translation catalog (no untranslated English leakage in the ko variant; no hardcoded strings) and en-US number formatting preserved.
- [ ] Both variants carry reciprocal `hreflang` (en, ko, x-default) and are each self-canonical to their own language URL. Verified on a fixture pair.
- [ ] The pool table / ranking / trust-rail gating is identical between the en and ko variants (same pools, same numbers) — translation is copy-only. Verified programmatically.
- [ ] KO sitemap coverage (own URLs or `xhtml:link` alternates). vercel.json/routing changes (if the subdirectory scheme needs them) flagged HIGH and confirmed not to disturb the `__APP_MODE` router or the sacred `?token=/?chain=/?pool=` behavior.
- [ ] Tests: assertions for hreflang reciprocity, ko-copy-from-catalog, and en/ko pool-parity, in the `npm test` chain.

## Measurement
Metric: GSC Korean-query impressions/clicks + `hreflang` validity (International Targeting report) once 026 unblocks. Window: 2–6wk+. Decision rule: keep if the hreflang validates and ko pages index; the traffic bet reads over a longer horizon.

## Risk tier (builder's guess — verifier assigns independently)
HIGH — generated SEO surface at 2× scale, user-facing copy in two languages, and a possible vercel.json/routing touch (router-adjacent = HIGH by NORTH_STAR). Large diff; verifier should confirm zero English leakage in ko variants and en/ko pool-parity (a divergence would be a trust/consistency failure).

## Open questions
- URL scheme (`/ko/...` subdirectory vs `?lang=ko` param) — the subdirectory is the SOTA recommendation but may need a vercel.json rewrite; if so, confirm with the human before building (router-adjacent). This is the one architecture-affecting choice — do not guess if the subdirectory scheme requires routing changes; default to the param scheme (no routing change) or ask.
