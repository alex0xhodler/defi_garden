# PR explainer — 067: fix $0 projection on capital-funded GROWTH plans

HIGH tier (NORTH_STAR.md risk policy: user-facing correctness fix on a core
headline number, not copy/styling — "when in doubt → HIGH"). Full
walkthrough + quiz per the ship policy's HIGH-tier requirement.

## Goal
A human-reported production bug: `/plan.html?goal=retirement&pace=rwa&capital=1000&fm=capital&years=5`
rendered "≈ $0 in 5 years", "vs $0 in a typical 0.5% savings account", and
"You'd have deposited $0 of your own money" — despite the sidebar correctly
showing `$1,000` capital and a live `10.58%` blended APY. This is the
product's headline trust number, on its default funding path (CLAUDE.md:
"Capital-first is the default funding path"), for one of three plan
archetypes (GROWTH — retirement, home). Fixed immediately per NORTH_STAR.md's
experiment discipline: "A bug at a funnel step outranks any experiment at
that step. Broken beats persuasion."

## Intuition
`futureValue(monthly, annualRatePct, years)` is a pure monthly-contribution
annuity formula — it has no notion of a one-time lump sum. A capital-funded
plan (`fm=capital`) has `monthly: null` (`decodePlanFromUrl`), so every call
site that computed GROWTH's headline via `futureValue(monthly, ...)` alone
silently evaluated to `$0` whenever a plan was capital-funded. TARGET and
SUBSCRIPTION archetypes already had their own capital-aware hero shapes
(`monthsUntilYieldCoversTarget`'s "flip date", `foreverNumber`'s "forever
progress") — GROWTH's dollar-projection shape was the one left with a
literal `// GROWTH: unchanged` comment at the exact line missing the capital
leg, a leftover from before the v3 capital-first funding mode existed.

The fix adds `capitalGrowth(capital, annualRatePct, years)` — lump-sum
compounding, `capital * (1+r)^years` — and adds it wherever GROWTH's dollar
projection is computed.

## What changed and why (diff in reading order)
- **`planner.js`**, new helper next to `futureValue`/`totalDeposited`:
  `capitalGrowth(capital, annualRatePct, years)`. Exported via the module's
  test-facing `api` object as `capitalGrowth`.
- **`buildPlanHero`**'s growth branch (and the unreachable-but-symmetric
  fallback branch): `futureValue(monthly, apy, years) + capitalGrowth(capital, apy, years)`.
  This feeds the persisted plan's `hero.projection` field.
- **Bloom's top-level `projection`/`bankProjection`/`deposited`** (feeds the
  `plan_saved` payload and the "was $X when planted" report line): each now
  adds `capitalGrowth(propCapital, ...)` — safe unconditionally, since
  `propCapital` is `null`/`0` for a monthly-funded plan.
- **Bloom's live-slider `liveProjection`/`liveBankProjection`** (the exact
  figures the reported URL renders): gated on `isCapitalPath` before reading
  `slideCapital` — see "a trap avoided" below for why the gate is required
  here but not at the two sites above.
- **The "You'd have deposited" line**: `isCapitalPath ? slideCapital : totalDeposited(slideMonthly, slideYears)` — a capital-funded plan's deposit is the lump sum itself, not a `monthly * months` product.
- **`GardenReport`'s `newProjection`** (the returning-visitor "your garden
  today" recompute, using `plan.capital`): same unconditional-add pattern as
  Bloom's top-level vars, since `plan.capital` is `null`/`0` when absent.
- **`test_planner.js`**: new unit tests for `capitalGrowth` in isolation, and
  for `buildPlanHero`'s growth branch under the exact reported scenario, plus
  an explicit "capital=0/null leaves a monthly plan unaffected" regression
  test.
- **`test_growth_capital_projection.js`** (new): Playwright test driving the
  real rendered `plan.html` with the *exact* reported URL, asserting no `$0`
  anywhere in the headline/bank-comparison/deposited lines and that the
  figure compounds above the $1,000 principal; a second assertion reloads the
  page (bare `/plan.html`, plan still in `localStorage`) to prove
  `GardenReport`'s independent recompute is fixed too.
- Regenerated `planner.min.js` via `npm run minify`, verified byte-identical
  via `test_minified_assets.js` (`plan.html` loads the minified file, not
  `planner.js` directly — the exact staleness gap 061 hit).

## A trap found and avoided mid-build
`slideCapital`'s `useState(propCapital || 5000)` defaults to `5000` even on a
plan with **no** capital (a monthly-funded plan). Adding
`capitalGrowth(slideCapital, ...)` to the live headline *unconditionally*
would have introduced a brand-new bug: every monthly-funded growth plan
would gain a phantom $5,000 added to its projection. Caught by reading the
`slideCapital` initializer before writing the fix (not by a failing test) —
a regression test was added afterward to lock the guard in.

## Investigated, not changed
The same bug report mentioned "amount in pool view." The referenced
screenshots are the same pool-detail page at two scroll positions: `TOTAL
APY 142.84%` at top, `30D MEAN APY 405.32%` further down. The "Calculate
Your Earnings" figures ($3.91/day, $119.03/mo on $1,000) verify exactly
against the 142.84% *total* APY — the calculator is using the intended
field. A 30-day mean diverging that far from the current rate is plausible
for a yield-farming reward pool (emission volatility), not a miscalculation.
No code change made; flagged back to the human as a possible future
UX/trust-copy question (should the page clarify why two very different APY
numbers appear together?), not fixed here since nothing was found broken.
