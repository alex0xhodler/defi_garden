## The bug, in one line

On the north-star surface, a pool whose rate is above the trust rail renders
**"$100 in this pool grows to ~$49,002,948,093,727,200,000 in 5y at current rates."**
The rate is flagged; the *fiction compounded from it* is not.

## Scope — what changes

When `isAnomalous` (`totalApy > APY_SANITY_LIMIT_LOCAL`, the existing constant, unchanged), the
pool-detail page must not render a **dollar amount derived from that rate**:

- **Projection card** (`PoolDetail.js:~633-636`): the `projectionBody` line is replaced by an
  honest out-of-range line. The card, its heading, the keep-note, the ⚠ warning and the disclaimer
  all stay where they are.
- **Daily + monthly quick-metric values** (`:697`, `:732`): render the placeholder `—` instead of
  the derived amount. Labels/sublabels unchanged.
- **Yield-calculator amount** (`:1095-1097`): same placeholder. The existing `calc-warning` at
  `:1108` already explains why and stays.

## Scope — what MUST NOT change (rails and controls)

- `APY_SANITY_LIMIT_LOCAL = 1000`, `DEFAULT_MIN_TVL`, the degen ⅓ haircut, `getRiskAssessment`'s
  anomalous→High override, `isAnomalous`, `showConcreteCta`: **read-only**. This item adds a
  render gate; it never relaxes a threshold. (Strengthening honesty, not weakening a rail — so
  not NEVER-list. Verifier: confirm independently.)
- **The pool's own reported rate keeps rendering** — hero APY (`:541-546`) and the Base/Reward APY
  cards (`:1210-1259`). The house convention is *demote + flag*, never hide the datum; and
  `test_audit_app.js` case 2 (the scanner's only P0 positive control, restored by item 155 after
  144 killed the previous one) injects `900,719,925,474,097.9` into `apyBase` and asserts a
  rendered `number-sanity` P0. **Suppressing those cards would kill that control a second time —
  do not touch them.** Re-run `test_audit_app.js` and prove it still passes.
- Healthy pools (`totalApy ≤ 1000`) must render byte-identically to `origin/main`.
- No new dependency. No change to `app.js`, `planner.js`, the generators, or any SEO surface.
