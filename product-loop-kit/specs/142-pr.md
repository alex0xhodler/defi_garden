## Fixture-trap discipline (why the scanner doesn't fabricate findings)
The playbook warns of three false-alarm classes; all three are guarded and verifier-probed:
1. **Snapshot staleness** — `**/data/pools-snapshot-meta.json` is routed with a fresh `generatedAt`, so the committed snapshot renders via the snapshot-first path instead of silently falling through to a live "0 results" that mimics a dead-end.
2. **Async meta** — every absence claim (empty-state, CTA, selector) polls up to ~10–12s before asserting (babel-standalone compile + fetch land seconds after DOM-ready).
3. **Money regex** — 1-decimal money is flagged only when NOT followed by a `[KMBT]` suffix, so `$11.2K`/`$273.3M` are legal and `$0.1` is the real 126-class bug.

## Deviations (all logged in 142-notes.md, all tooling-internal — no product code)
- `waitUntil: 'domcontentloaded'` + selector-polling (the blocked externals never fire `load` in-sandbox) — cut a full run from ~2m to ~18s.
- Absurd-magnitude threshold `1e11` instead of the playbook's literal `1e4`: a bare `>1e4` scan false-positives the legit `$100,000` calculator preset and 6-figure projections. `1e11` catches the entire −900T/9e14 class (verified) with zero false positives on real data. (Verifier noted the residual: a rate-context glitch between 1e4 and 1e11 would be missed since flat innerText can't separate money- from rate-context — accepted trade; the named 122 class is caught with wide margin.)
- Loading-flash check is structural (132's `.empty-submessage`-before-`.pool-card` signature), KO currency-truth is a byte-identical `원`/`$` compare (137 signature) — both low-false-positive and i18n-independent.
