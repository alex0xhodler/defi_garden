**Status:** IN_PROGRESS · **Score:** 7.0 · **Risk:** LOW · **North star:** pool-detail conversion clicks (`pool_click{source=garden_cta}` + `pool_click{source=protocol_link}`)

## Problem (documented evidence)
BACKLOG row 127 (P1, pairs with 122): under the calm rate-track-record note on the pool-detail page (the north-star conversion surface), item 117.1 added a SECOND line that dumps a raw analyst score:

> "Rate-stability score N (vs a ~4% risk-free rate): how steady this pool's yield has been relative to how much it pays — not a measure of principal safety."

For the cautious-retail-saver ICP this is jargon, not trust: a bare Sharpe-like number, a "risk-free rate" benchmark, and a "relative to how much it pays" risk-adjusted framing that the ICP does not think in. The backlog row: "keep the calm first sentence ('steady so far… easier to plan a garden around'), drop/rework the raw-Sharpe line for the cautious-saver ICP. 117.1 over-exposed the number; on the conversion page it reads as jargon, not trust." NORTH_STAR pre-traffic mandate (2026-07-23): the loop's job is product QUALITY on the rendered app — remove friction/jargon on the conversion page; clutter dilutes the CTA (sibling concern in 128/129).

## Decision — DROP the raw-score line (not a qualitative rework)
The backlog says "drop/rework". We DROP the annotation line entirely rather than rewording it, for three reasons:

1. **Honesty / trust-rail integrity.** A qualitative rework ("this pool's rate has been unusually steady for how much it pays") requires an invented threshold on `apySharpe` to decide what counts as "steady" — the note renders under BOTH the "Steady" and the volatile "Tracked" track-record variants, so a threshold-free qualitative sentence would assert steadiness even for low-Sharpe (unsteady) pools, which is dishonest. Inventing an unvalidated band is a bigger, riskier (arguably HIGH-tier) change; dropping invents nothing.
2. **The message is already delivered in ICP language.** The kept first sentence (`rateTrackRecordSteady`, unchanged) — "Steady so far… Steadier rates are easier to plan a garden around" — already carries the rate-stability signal for steady pools in plain words. The Sharpe line is redundant jargon on top of it.
3. **Surgical + reduces conversion-page clutter.** One render block + two dead translation keys removed. The `apySharpe` value's REAL job is untouched — it still drives the risk-adjusted SORT (117.2, app.js) and the planner tie-break (planner.js). Only the user-facing jargon LINE on pool-detail is removed.
