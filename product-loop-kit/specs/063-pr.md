# 063 — PR explainer (LOW tier)

## What shipped
Three new Mixpanel events fill the visibility gap between `waitlist_opened` and `waitlist_submitted` (009), across both waitlist entry points (bloom-moment modal + SEO quick-entry modal from 062):

- `waitlist_email_entered` — first keystroke in the email field (once per modal open)
- `waitlist_submit_attempt` — fires right before the Formspree POST, once client-side validation passes
- `waitlist_error({ reason })` — fires on every rejection: `empty_email`, `invalid_email`, `formspree_error`, `network`

No UX/copy/behavior change — pure instrumentation.

## Why
009 only sees the two endpoints of the waitlist funnel. Without the middle steps we can't tell whether people abandon at the email field, hit a validation error, or lose the network request — "you can't optimize a funnel you can't see." This unblocks the next waitlist conversion item (065/066) with real drop-off data instead of a guess.

## A real bug the tests caught
First implementation used `onFocus` for `waitlist_email_entered`. Both email inputs have `autoFocus: true`, so the browser's automatic mount-time focus fired the event immediately — *before* `waitlist_opened` even landed in the tracked queue. Every modal open would have logged "user entered their email" with zero user action, and the funnel order would read backwards. Fixed by switching to first `onChange` (genuine keystroke), verified via an explicit event-order assertion in the new Playwright test.
