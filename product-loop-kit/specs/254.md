## Hypothesis

We believe making every stating site **derive** its figure from `DEFAULT_MIN_TVL`, and re-pointing the
`tvl-floor-claim` signal at that constant, will keep the product's stated rail true across future rail
changes, because the defect's mechanism is *re-typing a rail into prose* and that mechanism is still
producing — two generators and four dictionary strings currently hold hand-typed copies of a number that
changed once and will change again. Confidence: **supported**. It pays off in every future where the floor
moves again, and in the meantime it repairs nine live false claims — so its payoff does not depend on any
unobserved condition (RAZOR's extension test), which is why it is scored rather than `GATED`.

- **Instance of:** a rail the product enforces in code, restated by hand in prose. The population is
  **every site that states a rail figure** — the TVL floor today, and `APY_SANITY_LIMIT` on exactly the
  same footing (`llms.txt:20` states `APY ≤ 1000%` from a hand-typed literal too; it is *currently correct*,
  which is why it belongs in the derivation, not in a bug list).
- **Class closed by this item: partially.** It closes the TVL-floor half and — because the derivation and
  the rail-relative check are written once — covers `APY_SANITY_LIMIT`'s stating sites at the same time.
  It does NOT close hand-typed *derived* figures (the `544 pools` and `3.2% blended rate` in `llms.txt`,
  which are generator output computed at whatever floor the generator holds). Those become correct as a
  consequence of fixing `MIN_TVL_USD`, but nothing then guards them; number left open and **not ticketed
  separately** — record it in the item's notes and let the next tick size it.

## Change

Smallest version that tests the hypothesis, in three legs:

1. **Single source of truth for the rail figures.** Export `DEFAULT_MIN_TVL` and `APY_SANITY_LIMIT` from
   one module the generators and the dictionary can both reach (the repo already has the shared-source
   pattern — see `test_llms_shared_source.js` and `test_seo_shared_source.js`; follow it rather than
   inventing a second one). Add a formatter that renders the constant as display text (`100000 → "$100K"`,
   `10000000 → "$10M"`) — `generate-llms.js:54` already contains exactly such a helper; reuse it, do not
   write a second.
2. **Convert the nine sites to derived values.** The four dictionary strings become functions taking the
   formatted floor (the dictionary already holds function leaves, so this needs no new mechanism — EN and
   KO together, per the hard rule); `home.html:238`, `generate-llms.js` and `generate-stories.js` take the
   formatted value from the shared export.
3. **Make `tvl-floor-claim` rail-relative.** Its predicate becomes: *a stated floor must equal
   `DEFAULT_MIN_TVL`*, evaluated at every stating site across every served surface — which strictly
   contains today's internal-consistency check. Keep the existing internal-consistency arm; add the
   rail-relative arm. Widening the surface set beyond the current 2 text surfaces is part of this leg: a
   check that reads only `llms.txt` and `llms-full.txt` would still be blind to sites 1–5 and 9.

**Explicitly OUT of scope:** the value of `DEFAULT_MIN_TVL` itself (human-owned, already decided, never
touched by this item); item 253's deleted key; item 255's source-level dictionary integrity gate; the
`$10M`-in-code-comments cleanup (`src/poller-core.js:48`, `app.js:2551`, `generate-llms.js:44`,
`PRODUCT.md:35`) beyond the lines the derivation edits anyway; and any change to which pools are shown.
