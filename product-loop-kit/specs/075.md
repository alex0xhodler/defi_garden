- 062 shipped the waitlist CTA (`seo_chain` source) on all ~86 EN + ~86 KO `/chains/<slug>` pages: today they carry the card-funnel CTA but NO concrete honest number strengthening the pitch — token pages (2,011×2) now do (066, verifier PASS HIGH 6/6, merged PR #182).
- North star = `waitlist_submitted` (card funnel); reports/2026-07-13.md: the SEO surface is the largest latent funnel entry and the pitch on those pages is what converts once GSC indexing (2–6wk, in progress) lands traffic. This closes the token/chain asymmetry on that surface.

## Change
- Reuse `yieldHeadlineFor(rec, lang)` from generate-token-pages.js AS-IS — it needs only `rec.pools` + lang, and chain records' `rec.pools` is already the displayed top-8-by-TVL set gated by `isQualifyingPool` (same trust rails: `MIN_POOL_TVL` floor, `APY_SANITY_LIMIT` anomaly exclusion) plus the 032-style visible-non-zero quality bar. Median (`gp.blendedApy`) over displayed pools, anchored to the Claude Pro ladder entry (`YIELD_HEADLINE_ANCHOR_ID`), exactly as 066 decided. Do NOT invent a new calc path; do NOT use the page's `bestApy` max.
- Generalize `renderYieldHeadlineHtml` minimally (css class + message key + subject params, or an equivalent small refactor) rather than duplicating it — 2026-07-10 reuse-before-inventing rule. Token pages' rendered output must stay byte-identical for identical input.
- New translation key `tcpYieldHeadlineChain(chain, apyStr, foreverAmtStr, monthly, subLabel)` in translations.js — EN + natural KO together, phrased for a chain ("idle assets on <Chain>" — a chain has no single token), mirroring `tcpYieldHeadline`'s honest framing ("could earn ~", "forever"). Run `npm run minify` after (translations.min.js staleness = 061's shipped bug; test_minified_assets.js gates it).
- Render in `renderChainPage` in the SAME slot as token pages (after intro, immediately before the `.cp-cta` link, above the table) with a `.cp-yield-headline` scoped style mirroring `.tp-yield-headline` — neumorphic tokens only.
- Null-headline honesty gate carries over unchanged: median rounding to "0.00%" or non-finite forever-number → no headline, never a fabricated/zero value.
- OUT of scope: spotlight packs (already carry the field per 066), token pages (beyond the non-behavioral helper generalization), hub pages, any backend, the card itself.
