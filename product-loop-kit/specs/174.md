# Spec 174: all 2,200 SEO pages publish a "DeFi Garden trust filter" that is 100× off

## Evidence
Measured 2026-07-29. The string appears on **every one** of the 2,200 committed SEO pages (88
`chains/*.html` + 2,112 `tokens/*.html`), inside the FAQ block, under the question *"Are these rates safe?"*.
Verbatim from `chains/cardano.html`:

> **Are these rates safe?** Every rate shown passes **DeFi Garden's trust filters — a $100K minimum TVL**
> and exclusion of anomalous (&gt;1000% APY) pools.

The product's floor is **$10M** — `app.js:801` `const DEFAULT_MIN_TVL = 10000000;`, and CLAUDE.md verbatim:
*"`DEFAULT_MIN_TVL = $10M` everywhere"*. The sentence does not scope the claim to the page ("pools on this
page clear $100K"); it asserts a property of **DeFi Garden's trust filters**, in answer to a safety question.
A reader — human or LLM — comes away believing our safety floor is 1/100th of what it is.

This is **item 159's defect, one surface over.** 159 was a P0 because `llms.txt` published
`TVL ≥ $10k` under a header claiming live opportunities while the product filtered at $10M. That was **2
files**. This is **2,200 pages**, and nobody re-ran the check on them after 159 shipped.

Two aggravating claims on the same pages, from the same generators, in scope for the same fix:

**(a) 1,713 pages state a "forever number" blended from pools the product refuses to show.** Cardano,
verbatim: *"Idle assets on Cardano could earn ~6.98% — park $3.4K and it could run a $20/mo Claude Pro
subscription, forever."* That blend includes a $715.9K pool. The forever number is the SUBSCRIPTION
archetype's trust-critical output — NORTH_STAR: *"forever numbers always from the live blended rate"*, and
CLAUDE.md: *"every displayed number derives from live DefiLlama pool data through sanity filters"*. Counts:
76 chain pages carry the claim (44 of them on pages whose own CTA returns zero pools), 2,036 token pages
(1,669 of them likewise).

**(b) 916 rows across 462 pages list a pool at `0.00%` APY** under a "DeFi Yields" heading — 142 rows / 53
pages on chains, 774 rows / 409 pages on tokens. E.g. `IUSD indigo → 0.00% $2.49M` on `chains/cardano.html`.
This is the 032/033 all-zero class: a yields page whose listed "yield" is zero is not a yield opportunity.

## Hypothesis
We believe **making each page describe the floor it actually applied, and sourcing its blended/forever
numbers only from pools that pass the product's rails, will remove a false safety claim from 2,200 indexed
pages** because the claim is currently a flat contradiction of `app.js:801` and of the rails CLAUDE.md
calls the moat. Confidence: **validated-pattern** — this is 159 re-run on a bigger surface, and 159's fix
shape (mirror the product's constants read-only, never re-type a literal, state the true floor) is known to
work and shipped clean.

## Change
Smallest version that removes the falsehood:
1. **Scope the claim to the truth.** Replace the FAQ sentence so it states the floor the page actually
   applied and does not attribute it to "DeFi Garden's trust filters" as a safety guarantee. The floor value
   must be interpolated from the generator's existing `MIN_POOL_TVL` constant — **never a second hardcoded
   `$100K` literal** (the 159 rule). The anomalous-APY half of the sentence is true and stays.
2. **Forever numbers honour the rails.** Either compute the blended rate used by the "could run a $X/mo
   subscription, forever" line from rail-passing pools only, or drop the claim on pages that have none.
   A forever number is a promise about capital; it may not rest on a pool the product will not display.
3. **`0.00%` rows.** Exclude zero-APY pools from the "DeFi Yields" table, or label them honestly as
   zero-yield rather than listing them as yields.
4. Regenerate `tokens/`, `chains/` and the sitemap suite via the npm scripts; commit the regenerated output.

**Every user-facing string added or changed goes through `translations.js`, EN + natural KO together** —
one language alone is a verifier FAIL (standing decision 2026-07-09).

**Explicitly OUT of scope:**
- Changing `MIN_POOL_TVL` itself, or rebuilding the pages at the $10M floor. That is a product-shape
  decision for the human (it would delete most of the 2,200-page surface) — flagged in the report, not
  assumed here.
- `DEFAULT_MIN_TVL`, `APY_SANITY_LIMIT`, anomaly flags, the degen haircut — **byte-untouched**.
- The dead CTAs on the same pages → item **173**. 174 fixes what the pages *say*; 173 fixes where they
  *go*. They touch the same two generators, so if both are in flight, land 173 first and rebase.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] `grep -rc "trust filters — a \$100K minimum TVL" chains/ tokens/` → **0** across all 2,200 pages.
- [ ] The replacement sentence names a floor that equals the generator's `MIN_POOL_TVL`, interpolated from
      the constant — verifier confirms by changing the constant in a scratch run and observing the copy
      change with it (no re-typed literal anywhere in the templates).
- [ ] No page attributes its page-level floor to "DeFi Garden's trust filters" in answer to a safety
      question.
- [ ] Every remaining "…subscription, forever" claim on every generated page derives from pools that pass
      `isQualifyingPool`-equivalent rails including the APY sanity limit; pages with no qualifying pool do
      not make the claim. Verifier re-derives the count with its own script.
- [ ] `0.00%` APY rows: either absent from the yields table, or rendered with an explicit zero-yield label.
      Report the before/after row count (baseline: 916 rows / 462 pages).
- [ ] EN + KO both updated in `translations.js` for every changed user-facing string; KO is natural, and any
      KO money figure carries the correct currency unit with no un-converted `원` relabel (the 137 trap).
- [ ] Tests: `node test_planner.js && node test_protocol_parsing.js && node test_qualifier_fix.js` green,
      plus `npm test`, plus a committed assertion covering the floor-claim string and the forever-number
      rail so this cannot regress (red→green non-vacuity proof in `specs/174-notes.md`).
- [ ] `app.js`'s `DEFAULT_MIN_TVL` / `APY_SANITY_LIMIT` lines byte-identical — verifier re-derives via
      `git diff`.
- [ ] SEO regen committed via the npm scripts; generated output never hand-edited.
- [ ] Instrumentation: none new (copy + number-provenance fix on a static surface).

## Measurement
Metric: no isolable funnel metric — this is an honesty fix on generated copy, like 159 and 070.
Window: **traffic-gated**, shared with 173: hold until **≥ 30 prod `page_view`s on `/tokens/*` or
`/chains/*`**. Read `search_success` / `pool_view` from static-SEO entry alongside 173, and note that the
two are confounded by design (same pages, same regen) — attribute nothing to 174 alone.
Decision rule: keep unconditionally. A false safety claim is not an experiment. Per NORTH_STAR's
minimum-sample rule, make no funnel claim below 30 events.

## Risk tier (builder's guess — verifier assigns independently)
**HIGH.** Regenerates the whole 2,200-page SEO surface, changes user-facing copy in two languages, and
touches the numbers behind a trust claim. Diff far exceeds LOW's 150-line cap. **Not** on the NEVER list:
no rail is weakened — the change makes the published claim match the rails, and constrains the forever
number *more* tightly than today. Nothing is de-indexed. When in doubt → HIGH, per policy.

## Open questions
One, and it does **not** block this item (the fix above is correct under either answer), but it should reach
the human because it is a product-shape call only they can make:
**should the `/tokens/` + `/chains/` surface be built at $100K at all, or rebuilt at the product's $10M
floor?** At $10M the surface collapses from 2,200 pages to roughly 450 — far less SEO surface, but every
page would then agree with the app by construction and 173 would become unnecessary. Ask; do not assume.
