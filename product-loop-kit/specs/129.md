# 129 — Lead the pool-detail page with the yield-funded frame, not the underwhelming daily/monthly $

Status: IN_PROGRESS · Score 6.0 · Risk MEDIUM (verifier assigns independently) · Author: build loop 2026-07-25

## Problem (documented evidence)
On the pool-detail page — the current north-star surface (`pool_click{source=garden_cta}` +
`pool_click{source=protocol_link}`) — the first numbers below the hero are the **Daily earnings** and
**Monthly earnings** stat cards (`PoolDetail.js` quick-metrics grid, ~L595–672). On a low-APY stable pool
(SUSDS, 3.6%) these read as "$0.10/day" and "$3/mo" — honest but underwhelming, and they *lead* the page.
The compelling, on-thesis number — the 5-year compounded projection ("The Long Game", ~L724–764) — renders
BELOW the earnings grid, so the underwhelming small-dollar figure is what frames the page before the CTA.

Per the yield-funded thesis (CLAUDE.md), the honest headline frame is: *you keep your capital AND it grows*
— not "you'll earn ten cents a day". The projection is the number that carries that frame.

## Goal
Lead the pool-detail body with the 5-year projection and its "keep your money" framing, so the first thing
a scroller sees below the hero is the compelling honest number, not "$0.10/day". Honest numbers intact — no
fabrication, all trust rails (anomaly gating, degen ⅓ haircut, disclaimers) preserved verbatim.

## Change (smallest that satisfies the goal)
1. **Reorder (DOM):** move the existing "The Long Game" projection card so it renders IMMEDIATELY AFTER the
   hero card and BEFORE the quick-metrics grid (daily / monthly / risk). No content of either block changes;
   the daily/monthly cards stay exactly as-is (they remain the single earnings surface per 128), just below
   the projection instead of above it.
2. **Distinctive class:** add `pool-projection-card` to the projection card's className (currently
   `metric-card-simple animate-on-mount`) so the reorder is targetable by the acceptance test and future work.
   No new CSS rule — the class is a hook only; visual styling continues to come from `metric-card-simple`.
3. **Keep-your-money line:** add ONE honest lead line to the projection card reinforcing the thesis — the
   deposit stays the user's (self-custodial, withdrawable — the product's stated premise) and keeps working.
   New translation key `projectionKeepNote`, EN + natural KO together. Reuses the existing thesis wording
   already in translations.js ("you keep your money", L433/499/511). No numbers in this line → nothing to rail.

Non-goals: no change to the daily/monthly/risk cards' content, no change to the calculator, no new CSS rule,
no change to any CTA, event, projection math, anomaly/degen gating, or trust rail. Not conditional on APY —
leading with the projection is the honest headline for every pool, not a per-pool hack (a threshold would be
arguable and add branching).
