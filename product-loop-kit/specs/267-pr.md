**Impact:** agent-contract correctness on API, MCP, and x402 documentation surfaces

## Why

`PRICE_SCHEDULE` controlled payment behavior, but four prose contracts independently named today's
free/paid split. A future route change could leave agents reading stale pricing documentation.

## What changed

- `x402-core.js` derives route/tool Markdown and the machine boundary sentence from the live
  schedule.
- `generate-pricing-docs.js` rewrites one marked region in each public pricing document.
- `X402.md` and `API.md` list every schedule route; `MCP.md` lists every real tool through its
  declared route.
- `test_x402_core.js` checks all marker populations and mutates one tier to prove the machine
  boundary moves with the schedule.

No price, tier, availability state, protocol path, dependency, or product UI changed.
