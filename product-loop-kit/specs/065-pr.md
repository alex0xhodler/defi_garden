# 065 — Waitlist value-prop framings: pitch variants, instrumented (LOW tier — short explainer)

## What this is
The waitlist modal (both the Bloom modal and the 062 quick modal) now supports three honest card-pitch framings:

- **A (default, unchanged)** — 061's copy: "Get early access to the card". Rendered byte-identically whenever `pitch` is absent or invalid, so 061's measurement window (through 2026-07-26) stays clean.
- **B (`?pitch=b`)** — the "flip" angle: "Your subscriptions, paid by your yield" — pay normally and the money's gone; the card pays from yield and the deposit stays yours.
- **C (`?pitch=c`)** — the ownership angle: "Yield pays the bill — the money stays yours" — deposit never spent, withdraw anytime.

All copy EN+KO together; every variant states the card doesn't exist yet (no fake availability), no scarcity/urgency, no hardcoded yield numbers.

## How it works
- `planner.js`: module-scope `PITCH_VARIANT` read once from the `pitch` URL param (`b`/`c` case-insensitive, else `a`) + `pitchKey()` mapping `waitlistTitle`/`waitlistBenefits` to their variant keys at the six render sites (title, benefits, dialog aria-label × two modals).
- `analytics.js`: all five waitlist trackers (`waitlist_opened/_email_entered/_submit_attempt/_submitted/_error`) now carry `pitch_variant`; every planner call site passes it. Winner is pickable on submit-rate segmented by `pitch_variant` (≥30 opened per variant, else "insufficient data").
- `translations.js`: 8 added keys, zero modified keys (`git diff --numstat` = 8/0).
- Regenerated `planner.min.js`/`translations.min.js` committed (plan.html loads the .min files). `analytics.js` is loaded raw by both HTML shells — no analytics.min.js exists (spec §4 listed one in error; documented in 065-notes.md, verifier-confirmed).
