# Spec 168: the AI-discovery surface must describe the Garden Planner — the product's default face

## Evidence

Fetched from **live prod by curl on 2026-07-28** (`signals/2026-07-28.md`, finding 168).

```
$ grep -icE "planner|garden plan|savings|goal|subscription|forever number" llms.txt
0
```

Zero. The file's own description of what DeFi Garden is (`llms.txt` lines 1-10, verbatim):

```
# Find the Best Yields for Your Tokens Across All Chains | DeFi Garden
...
## Homepage
TL;DR: Main dashboard for discovering DeFi yields across all chains and protocols.
- https://www.defi.garden/
```

`plan.html` appears **exactly once** in `llms-full.txt` — line 2799, a bare URL inside a flat list of 4,917
URLs, with no description, no TL;DR, and no section of its own.

Against the product's own definition of itself:
- CLAUDE.md: *"Garden Planner (the DEFAULT feature, bare `/` and `plan.html`) — a goal-first, conversational,
  generative-UI savings planner."*
- NORTH_STAR 2026-07-12 (business model): *"the planner's SUBSCRIPTION archetype / forever-number flip is the
  product's front door."*
- NORTH_STAR ICP: *"the cautious retail saver who thinks in monthly deposits and life goals, NOT in
  APY/pools. Not the degen (they use DefiLlama directly)."*

So the one artifact whose entire purpose is telling LLMs and agents what this product is currently teaches
them that it is a DefiLlama-style yield screener — the exact positioning the product exists not to be, and
the one where a DefiLlama-shaped competitor already wins.

## Hypothesis

We believe **adding an honest, data-grounded planner section to `llms.txt`/`llms-full.txt`** will
**make the product citable for the queries our ICP actually asks** ("how do I save for X", "can yield pay my
subscription", "how much do I need to cover a $23/mo bill"), because an LLM can only cite what our surface
says exists, and right now our surface says only "yields, by token and chain". Confidence: **supported**
that the surface misdescribes the product (verbatim, zero mentions); **guess** on the citation effect — AI
citation is not measurable in-loop today (no GSC connector until ~2026-08-23 per the 2026-07-12 decision,
`src` breakdown returns `undefined` only).

## Change

In `generate-llms.js` only — a new section in both files, plus a corrected homepage TL;DR:

1. **Fix the homepage row's TL;DR** so it names both faces: the goal-first Garden Planner at bare `/` AND
   the yield analytics app on parameterized URLs. One sentence, no marketing adjectives.
2. **Add a `## Garden Planner` section** to `llms.txt` (short) and `llms-full.txt` (fuller), containing:
   - what it is in one line (goal-first savings planner for people who think in monthly deposits, not APY);
   - the three plan archetypes named and one-lined — GROWTH (retirement/home), TARGET (a specific item),
     SUBSCRIPTION (the "forever number": the capital whose yield pays a recurring bill);
   - `${meta.baseUrl}/plan.html` as the entry URL, plus one or two **routed** preset URLs built from real
     `PLANNER_PARAMS` (`home.html:78`: `goal, monthly, years, pace, preset, fresh, capital, fm, dl`);
   - a **live blended rate** derived from the same trust-rail-passing pool set the rest of the file already
     uses, so any number in the section comes from `pickHighYield()`-eligible data and inherits 159's rails.
3. **Add a query-example line or two** in the existing "Typical user queries" section for planner-shaped
   intents, pointing at the planner, not the grid.

**Honesty constraints — these are acceptance criteria, not style notes:**
- **No forever-number or projection figure may be hardcoded or invented.** Every number in the new section
  derives from the rail-passing live pool set, exactly like the rest of the file. If the rate is
  unavailable, the section must omit the number, not estimate one.
- **The card does not exist yet.** Per the 2026-07-12 standing decision, any mention of the subscription card
  must read as early-access/waitlist framing — no fake availability.
- Copy ban-list for in-reach goals applies (CLAUDE.md): no "save up", "afford", "budget".

**Explicitly OUT of scope:**
- Link targeting of the existing pool/protocol rows — that is item **166**, which ships first.
- `translations.js` / KO. `llms*.txt` is a single English machine-readable artifact with no KO counterpart;
  this adds no user-facing UI string, so the EN+KO rule is not engaged. **Say so explicitly in
  `168-pr.md`** — a reviewer should see the rule was checked, not skipped.
- Any change to the planner itself, `plan.html`, `planner.js`, or the router.
- `sitemap-*.xml`, token/chain pages, `stories/`, OG images.

## Measurement

Metric: planner arrivals — `session_start` / `page_view` on `/` and `/plan.html`, and `plan_created` per
planner session (the existing guardrail).
Window: **gated on a traffic precondition, not a date** (2026-07-27 LEARNINGS takeaway). Decision rule:
evaluate when cumulative planner `page_view` ≥ 30 in a rolling 14d window (today: 1 in 7d). AI-citation
effect is not in-loop measurable before the human's GSC read (~2026-08-23).
Build leg is measurable today and is the acceptance criteria above.
Minimum sample per NORTH_STAR.md.
