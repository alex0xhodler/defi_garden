# Spec 231: the `occlusion` lens must not report a clean page it never actually measured

## Evidence

All numbers below are quoted, not re-derived; provenance in brackets.

- `audit-app.js`'s `occlusion` lens flagged the `dead-pool` surface in **3 of 20** TRUE
  single-process isolated `runAudit({ only: ['dead-pool'] })` runs on unmodified `main`
  (two independent batches: 2/10, then 1/10 on a fresh batch with fresh ports), each red run
  carrying the **identical** victim geometry — `<div class="tvl-label">` "TVL"
  `{x:428.1, y:718.8, w:23.1, h:15.7}` vs `.app-footer` `{x:0, y:711, w:1280, h:69}`, 100%
  covered, hit-test resolving to the footer, +4 more occluded elements on the same pass.
  [`specs/221-notes.md`, "CORRECTION (operator/verifier review, attempt 2)"]
- A throwaway diagnostic mirroring `checkOcclusion`'s exact sequence (initial viewport 900 tall,
  resize to `OCCLUSION_HEIGHT=780`, 150 ms settle, `domcontentloaded` not `load`) reproduced that
  same fully-settled occluded geometry on **~14 of 14** runs across three script iterations,
  **never once catching a clean state**. [`specs/221-notes.md`]
- Item 230 then reproduced the same defect **10 of 10** with its own independent instrument
  (real Chromium, `test_dead_pool.js`'s fixture pattern, at rest). [BACKLOG row 230]
- Two rival explanations were RULED OUT by 221 with direct probes: the **CSS-load race**
  (15/15 probe runs had `style.min.css` already applied — `link.media === 'all'`, `.app-footer`
  computed `position: fixed` — at the instant the driver's own wait condition resolved) and
  **content variance** (`getEmptyStateAlternatives`, `app.js:2517-2528`, is a deterministic
  top-5 pick from an in-memory snapshot). [`specs/221-notes.md`]
- The lens visits **83 surfaces per tick** (audit `2026-08-04T07:13:34Z`), and the
  *fixed-overlay-occlusion* class has produced **six** items — 179 → 217 → 218 → 220 → 221 → 230.
  [BACKLOG rows 221/230/231]

*The page is reliably broken; only the DETECTION is intermittent.* That is the defect this item
fixes — not a pixel.

**State of the original positive control, stated up front because it changes the test design:**
item 230 SHIPPED the `dead-pool` fix (`591db37748`), so the 3/20 surface is now clean on `main`
and can no longer serve as a live control. Two replacements are used instead, both defined under
Acceptance: (a) a fixture that reproduces the *mechanism* with a randomised late layout change,
and (b) a live positive-control set **derived at test time** from the audit's own surface list —
`main` still carries the unfixed `.app.has-results` grid occlusion (221 measured
`calculate-yield-btn-new` **98.8%** covered at 1280×780; its fix is PARKED behind unmerged PR #386).

## Hypothesis

We believe **replacing the lens's fixed wall-clock settle with an asserted, converged
pre-measurement state** will raise its detection rate on a permanently-occluded surface from
~15% to ≥95%, because `checkOcclusion` currently measures at one arbitrary instant
(`page.setViewportSize(...)` then `await page.waitForTimeout(150)`, `audit-app.js:4195-4202`)
and never verifies either precondition its measurement depends on:

1. **that the new viewport was actually applied.** Every occlusion this lens exists to catch is
   height-dependent by construction (`OCCLUSION_HEIGHT = 780` is the exact height at which 218's
   defect appears; at the 900 the surfaces are rendered at, the same bottom-fixed footer clears
   the same victims — the lens's own constant comment says so, `audit-app.js:342-353`). If the
   renderer has not applied the resize when `occlusionPassEval` reads `window.innerHeight`, the
   footer is measured at the *900-tall* position, intersects nothing, and the pass returns a
   clean result that is a **false negative by construction**.
2. **that layout has converged.** A React surface re-renders on resize; a surface whose final
   layout lands after the 150 ms mark is measured mid-flight.

Both failure modes produce exactly the observed signature: identical geometry whenever it *is*
seen, a silent clean whenever it is not, and a manual probe (less loaded, resize applied
promptly) that never reproduces the clean state.

Confidence: **supported** — 221 characterised the race and ruled out the two rival mechanisms it
could test, but could not pin the exact instant the harness's clean runs were reading. This item
therefore does NOT claim to have identified *the* mechanism; it adopts the weakest fix consistent
with the evidence: **never measure a page whose measured preconditions are unverified, and never
let an unverified skip read as clean** (RAZOR side 2).

**Scoping of that claim, corrected after attempt 1 — the verifier falsified the unqualified
version:** "never measure an unverified page" is only as wide as the instrument that decides what
counts as *verified*. A **sampling** rule is satisfied by a page that merely happens to be quiet at
the instant you look, which is why attempt 1 scored **20/20** on continuously-settling pages and
**0/10** on pages that go quiet and then make one late change — reported clean, with no P2, because
from the poll's point of view convergence genuinely held. The revised form (§Change 2) **observes
mutations instead of sampling for stillness**, widening the covered class to any DOM- or
resize-observable late change; criterion **1c** then requires this item to go looking for what is
*still* outside that class and print the number. No claim is made here about causes no instrument
in this item can witness.

- **Instance of:** *a detector whose evidence is a single sample taken at a fixed wall-clock
  offset*. Population, enumerated on `main` (`grep -n "waitForTimeout(" audit-app.js` → **4 call
  sites**, classified rather than counted): `:3464` is `pollFor`'s own poll interval (not a
  measurement gate); `:4202` is this item's target; `:4253` is the occlusion lens's pass-2 settle,
  already guarded by a `scrollHeight`-stability condition and left as-is per §Change; `:3613` is a
  flat `await page.waitForTimeout(400)` in the **`static`-kind surface driver**, immediately before
  `auditText(page, s, findings)`.
- **Class closed by this item: no — with the number.** `:3613` gates the text/number audit of
  **every `static`-kind surface**, which is the bulk of the lens's 83 surfaces per tick, on exactly
  the same unverified assumption this item removes from the occlusion lens: that a fixed sleep
  means the page is done. Its false-negative rate has **never been measured** — this item does not
  measure it and does not claim to. Ticketed as **232** (filed in BACKLOG by this item):
  *"`audit-app.js:3613` — the static-surface driver still measures after a flat 400 ms sleep;
  measure its false-negative rate the way 231 measured the occlusion lens's, then convert it to an
  asserted/converged wait if the rate warrants."*

## Change

`audit-app.js` `checkOcclusion()` only. **Zero product-file diff** — no `app.js`, `home.html`,
`planner.js`, `style.css`, `PoolDetail.js` change; no trust rail, router, or SEO surface touched.

1. **Assert the viewport before measuring.** After `setViewportSize`, poll (bounded, ~2000 ms)
   until `window.innerWidth === s.width && window.innerHeight === OCCLUSION_HEIGHT`. If it never
   holds: push a **P2 advisory** naming the measured values and **skip the passes** — the lens's
   standing doctrine is that a check must never fail silently (`audit-app.js:4188-4192`), and a
   measurement at the wrong viewport is a lie, not a clean.
2. **Wait for a QUIET DWELL measured from DWELL START — observe, do not sample, and never let the
   page's age decide the answer.**
   *(REVISED AGAIN for attempt 3 — attempt 2's verifier FAIL. Attempt 2 anchored its floor to
   `performance.now()` since NAVIGATION (`MIN_PAGE_AGE_MS = 500`). Measured consequence: real
   surfaces sit on both sides of that line (`landing` 196 ms, `pool-detail` 408 ms, `grid-token`
   447 ms vs `planner` 1,155 ms, `static-page` ~13,000 ms), so the floor is neither the no-op the
   notes claimed nor uniformly protective — on an already-old page the mechanism degrades to the
   `QUIET_MS`-only design the builder had already measured at 7/20, and the verifier reproduced
   **8/20** there. Meanwhile the 2,000 ms budget, re-armed indefinitely by `AnimatedNumber`'s rAF
   churn, pushed a real `runAudit` leg to **+23.4%**, over the ceiling. One change fixes both: make
   every timer relative to DWELL START, and cap the tail.)*
   **The rule, in full:** watch until `max(MIN_WATCH_MS, last observed change + QUIET_MS)`, capped at
   `BUDGET_MS`, **every term measured from the moment the dwell starts — page age is not an input.**
   Starting values to measure and then justify with the numbers, not to assume:
   `MIN_WATCH_MS = 400`, `QUIET_MS = 150`, `BUDGET_MS = 900`.
   - `MIN_WATCH_MS` is the uniform floor that replaces `MIN_PAGE_AGE_MS`. It fixes criterion 1d
     because it does not care how old the page is: a single late change landing within 400 ms of the
     lens starting is observed and re-arms the dwell on young and old pages alike.
   - `BUDGET_MS = 900` caps the churn tail that broke criterion 7. Bounding it is not a compromise
     of correctness so much as an honest statement of horizon — but the horizon SHRINKS, so it must
     be measured against criteria 1/1b/1d, not assumed harmless.
   - Both numbers are budget-coupled: roughly `+250 ms × ~83 surfaces ≈ +21 s` on a 238 s leg
     (≈ +9%) for the floor, plus whatever the churny grid surfaces spend up to the cap. Measure it;
     if the pair cannot satisfy criteria 1/1b/1d AND criterion 7 together, that is a real result —
     PARK with the numbers and let the human decide whether the audit may cost more.
   **Mechanism (unchanged from attempt 2, and verifier-confirmed load-bearing):**
   *(REVISED after attempt 1's verifier FAIL — see §Attempt history. The first implementation used
   a two-consecutive-identical-samples poll; that predicate is satisfied by any page that is merely
   quiet at the moment you look, so a page quiescent for >100 ms that then makes ONE late layout
   change was detected **0 of 10** times — measured by the verifier, not argued. A sampling rule
   cannot see a change that lands between its exit and its measurement; the weakest form is to stop
   sampling and start **observing**.)*
   Install a `MutationObserver` (childList + subtree + attributes) and a `ResizeObserver` (on
   `documentElement` and on every visible `fixed`/`sticky` element — the overlay set the lens's own
   verdict depends on) inside the page, and wait until **`QUIET_MS` = 150 ms elapse with no observed
   mutation or resize**, bounded by a total budget of ~2000 ms. Every observed change **re-arms** the
   dwell.
   Cost note, and the reason this form is cheap as well as weak: on an already-quiet page it exits
   after one dwell — the same ~150 ms today's flat sleep pays — while a change at 300 ms re-arms and
   exits at ~450 ms. It is strictly wider than the two-sample rule (it covers ramps, single late
   changes, and any DOM/geometry mutation) at essentially unchanged cost.
   If the budget is exhausted while the page is still churning, push a **P2 advisory** naming the
   last observed change **and measure anyway**: an occlusion seen on an unsettled page is still
   positive evidence, while its absence is not evidence of health. (Asymmetry deliberate; it is the
   same asymmetry the file already applies elsewhere.)
   **Residual class, to be measured and stated, not assumed away:** a geometry change driven purely
   by the compositor with neither a DOM mutation nor an observed resize. `ResizeObserver` does fire
   on CSS-transitioned/animated size changes, so the expected residual is narrow — but the item must
   report what it measured rather than reason about it.
3. Keep pass 2's existing bottom-of-scroll settle loop as-is (it already gates on `scrollHeight`
   stability). Keep `OCCLUSION_HEIGHT` / `OCCLUSION_MIN_COVERAGE` / `OCCLUSION_CANDIDATE_CAP`
   byte-unchanged.

**Explicitly OUT of scope:** fixing any product occlusion the improved lens now finds (a
detector's new findings are the next tick's tickets — 219's own policy); the 2 non-occlusion
fixed sleeps (item 232); the `QUARANTINED_OCCLUSION_SURFACES` set in `test_audit_app.js` (221's
territory, PARKED behind PR #386); raising `DEFAULT_POOL_LENS_SAMPLE` or any other budget knob.

**Cost budget (must be measured, not assumed):** the convergence poll must not blow the audit's
foreground cap. On a settled surface it should return after the second sample (~100-150 ms),
i.e. within noise of today's flat 150 ms. Acceptance requires the measured
before/after wall-clock of a real `runAudit` leg to be recorded in the notes, with a **+20%
ceiling** on that leg's duration.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] **1. Detection rate — the item's own definition, measured, not asserted.** New test
      `test_audit_occlusion_detection_rate.js` drives `checkOcclusion()` against a fixture whose
      occlusion is **permanent but final only after a randomised late layout change**, delay drawn
      per run from a **seeded uniform distribution over [0, 400] ms** (spanning both sides of the
      old 150 ms threshold; seeded by run index so a failure is reproducible). **20 isolated runs
      → ≥19 report the occlusion (≥95%).**
- [ ] **1d. The THIRD fixture family — the one attempt 2 shipped blind to: an OLD page.** Attempt 2's
      floor was anchored to *page navigation*, so every fixture (which calls `page.setContent()` and
      invokes `checkOcclusion` immediately) only ever exercised the young-page branch. The verifier
      aged a page past the floor before invoking the real, unmutated `checkOcclusion`, fired ONE late
      DOM-mutating change at the same seeded [0,400] ms distribution, and measured **8/20 (40%)** —
      attempt 1's failure magnitude, for ordinary DOM mutations, on the surface class the item's own
      text calls "the bulk of the 83" (real measured ages at dwell-start: `static-page` ~13,000 ms,
      `planner` 1,155-1,412 ms, `grid-token` 447-494 ms, `pool-detail` 408-416 ms, `landing`
      196-211 ms — so BOTH branches occur in production and the floor is not a no-op either way).
      Requirement: the single-late-change family, run against a page **aged past every floor before
      `checkOcclusion` is invoked**, detects **≥19/20**. The 8/20 above is this criterion's positive
      control. A mechanism whose detection rate depends on how old the page happens to be has not
      fixed the class — it has moved it.
- [ ] **1b. The SECOND fixture family — the one attempt 1 shipped blind to.** The same 20-run
      harness against a fixture that is **quiescent for >100 ms and then makes ONE single,
      instantaneous late layout change** (no ramp), seeded delays over the same [0, 400] ms
      distribution: **≥19/20 detect it.** Attempt 1 measured **0/10** on this family; that number is
      the positive control for this criterion, and the pre-fix control sequence must be run against
      this family too and reported. A fix that passes criterion 1 but not 1b has re-shipped attempt
      1's overclaim.
- [ ] **1c. The residual class is measured and named.** State in the notes what shape of late
      change, if any, still evades the observer-based dwell, with the run count behind the claim.
      "None found" is an acceptable answer only if an attempt was actually made to find one.
- [ ] **2. The fixture carries a REAL permanent defect, proven — not a fixture that only looks
      broken.** Before the rate leg, the test settles the same fixture fully (convergence + 1000 ms
      extra) and asserts the occlusion is present on **5 of 5** samples. A fixture that cannot be
      shown permanently occluded fails the test loudly rather than making the rate leg meaningless.
- [ ] **3. Old sequence is discriminated — the fixture is not fitted to the fix.** The same
      20-run harness driving the PRE-FIX sequence (fixed 150 ms sleep, no viewport assertion, no
      convergence poll) must detect **≤ 14/20**, materially below the new rate; the test asserts
      `newHits > oldHits` and `oldHits <= 14`, and prints both numbers.
- [ ] **4. Live positive control, population derived at test time.** The test asks the audit's own
      surface list for candidates, runs a *settled reference probe* on each grid surface
      (`grid-token`, `grid-chain`, `grid-360`), and forms the live positive-control set **P** =
      those that show ≥1 occlusion when fully settled. If **P is empty the test FAILS loudly**
      ("no live positive control available — detection rate on a real surface is unmeasurable
      this run"), never passes vacuously. For the first surface in P: **5 of 5** isolated
      `checkOcclusion` runs report an occlusion. Timebox: if this leg exceeds 5 foreground
      minutes, record what was achieved in the notes and state it as an incomplete leg — never
      report it as green.
- [ ] **5. Never silent.** Source-level assertion (no browser) that both new skip/degrade paths
      push a `'occlusion'` P2 finding, and a rendered assertion that a page which cannot reach the
      target viewport produces that P2 rather than a clean pass.
- [ ] **6. No regression.** `node test_audit_occlusion_lens.js` (24/24 on `main` this run) and
      `node test_audit_app.js` both still pass, unchanged. The new test is registered in
      `package.json`'s `test:serial` and in whatever registry `test_test_registry.js` enforces.
- [ ] **7. Cost — measured on a REAL `runAudit` leg, not on a fixture test file.** Attempts 1 and 2
      both reported the wall-clock of `test_audit_occlusion_lens.js` (+7.6%), which is a file of
      synthetic fixtures and NOT what this criterion asks for. The verifier measured the real thing —
      `runAudit({ poolLiveness: false })` over the full default surface population, base worktree vs
      branch, twice each: **238.68 s → 294.43 s = +23.4%**, breaching the +20% ceiling on both runs.
      Root cause, confirmed: `app.js`'s `AnimatedNumber` (`app.js:750`, 3× per pool card with
      staggered delays) re-renders text via `requestAnimationFrame` for up to ~1.2 s, continuously
      re-arming the `MutationObserver` and driving `grid-token`'s dwell to `elapsedMs=1854` and then
      to budget exhaustion at `elapsedMs=2004`. Requirement: measure the same real leg the same way,
      before and after, and land **≤ +20%**. Record both raw numbers. If the mechanism cannot be both
      correct (1/1b/1d) and within budget, PARK with the measured numbers — shipping a smaller
      improvement as a solved class is the failure mode this criterion exists to prevent.
- [ ] **Non-vacuity (mutation proof, run in-session, recorded with output):** each new sub-rule
      neutered **separately** — (a) viewport assertion removed, (b) convergence poll replaced by
      the old flat `waitForTimeout(150)` — and for each: gate RED, then byte-identical restore
      (`md5sum` before/after quoted), then gate GREEN. "Three working rules" must be
      distinguishable from "one working rule and one dead one"; if a sub-rule does not go red, say
      so explicitly and state why it still ships (222's precedent), never quietly.
- [ ] **Population:** the fixture's delays are drawn from a distribution, never one constant; the
      live control set is derived from the audit's surface list at test time, never a hardcoded
      surface name that could be silently fixed out from under the test (exactly what happened to
      `dead-pool` between this item being filed and being built).

## Measurement

Metric: **the occlusion lens's detection rate on a known-permanently-occluded surface**
(pre-fix: 3/20 = 15%, measured; target ≥95%). This is loop tooling, not a user-facing funnel —
there is **no traffic gate and no ≥30-event rule to apply**; per NORTH_STAR's
"calendar windows only for build-leg verification", this is a pure build leg, verified in-session
by criteria 1-4. Decision rule: if the rate leg cannot be brought ≥19/20 within the 3-attempt
budget, PARK with the measured rate rather than shipping a smaller improvement as a solved class.

Downstream (not claimed here): every "0 blocking findings" tick becomes evidence proportional to
the lens's real detection rate — the point of RAZOR side 1. This item does not claim to have
made past green ticks trustworthy; it makes future ones interpretable.

## Risk tier (builder's guess — verifier assigns independently)

**LOW.** `audit-app.js` is loop tooling with no product render path, zero product-file diff, no
new dependency, no trust rail, no SEO surface, no `home.html` router touch. The one asymmetry
worth naming: a lens that now flags more will make future audit ticks noisier — deliberately, and
the quarantine mechanism for pre-existing reds already exists in `test_audit_app.js`.

## Territory notes

- `checkOcclusion` is already exported (`audit-app.js:4860`) specifically so
  `test_audit_occlusion_lens.js` can drive it directly against a hand-made fixture — the new test
  reuses that entry point and that file's fixture/server machinery rather than inventing a second
  harness (reuse-before-inventing, standing decision 2026-07-10).
- `pollFor(page, fn, timeoutMs)` (`audit-app.js:3458`) already exists and is the house pattern for
  bounded condition waits — the new waits use it, not a new helper.
- `occlusionPassEval` reads `window.innerWidth/innerHeight` directly (`audit-app.js:4057`) and
  derives `bottomAnchor` from `viewportH`; this is precisely why an unapplied resize yields a
  structurally clean pass, and why criterion 1 is a *precondition assertion*, not a longer sleep.
- The verifier should note that `dead-pool` — the surface every number in Evidence was measured
  on — is FIXED on `main` as of `591db37748`. Any attempt to re-measure the 3/20 baseline on
  `dead-pool` today will (correctly) find nothing; that is not a contradiction of the evidence.
