# 068 — PR explainer (HIGH tier): planner hero-copy CRO — outcome headline, mechanism tagline, live-rate proof anchor

## Goal
The planner hero (bare `/`, the top of the `waitlist_submitted` funnel) was feature-named: "Garden Planner / Plant a goal. Watch it grow." An external CRO audit (SPLITSTREAM, 2026-07-13) flagged that the ICP — a cautious retail saver — converts on outcomes and concrete live numbers, not on product names and metaphors. Every funnel entrant reads this copy before picking a goal, so a lift here compounds through the whole funnel.

## Intuition
Three SPLITSTREAM variants, shipped together as one hero:
- **V01 (outcome)**: the h1 now names what the product does for you — "Pay your bills with yield, forever" — instead of what the product is called.
- **V02 (mechanism)**: the tagline explains *how* in one breath — "Tell us what you need. Live DeFi yield covers it automatically."
- **V03 (proof)**: the splash hook now carries a **live-derived** blended rate ("at today's X% blended rate…") instead of a numberless claim. The audit's literal suggestion was a hardcoded "8-15% APY … within months" — that would have violated the trust rails (fabricated number + fabricated timeframe), so the shipped version derives the rate from the same trust-railed calculation the product already uses everywhere else, and drops the timeframe entirely (at splash time no subscription or monthly amount is chosen, so any time-to-cover claim would be invented).

## What changed and why (diff in reading order)
1. **translations.js** — EN (~:123-135) and KO (~:710-720) together, per the hard rule:
   - `title`: "Garden Planner" → "Pay your bills with yield, forever" · KO "수익이 요금을 내줘요 — 영원히"
   - `tagline`: "Plant a goal. Watch it grow." → "Tell us what you need. Live DeFi yield covers it automatically." · KO "필요한 걸 알려주세요. 실시간 DeFi 수익이 알아서 내드려요."
   - NEW function-valued key `splashHookLive(apy)` (the `blendedBadge` pattern): EN "Park money once — at today's ${apy} blended rate, the yield pays your bill forever and you keep every dollar." · KO "한 번 넣어두면 — 오늘 기준 혼합 수익률 ${apy}로 수익이 요금을 영원히 내주고, 원금은 고스란히 남아요."
   - The static `splashHook` is **untouched in both languages** — it is the honest, number-free degrade path.
2. **planner.js** — exactly one line (:4002, the goal-step splash hook):
   `stableGuidanceApy ? t('splashHookLive', formatApy(stableGuidanceApy)) : t('splashHook')`
   `stableGuidanceApy` (:3557) = `blendedApy(curatePools(pools,'stable',3))`, computed only when the pools fetch is ready — i.e. the median APY of the top stable-persona pools after the full trust rails (`APY_SANITY_LIMIT` 1000%, stable band TVL floor $50M, anomaly exclusion; no degen haircut applies to the stable persona). It is `null` until live data exists, so the hook can never show a placeholder or stale figure. The tempting-but-wrong alternative, `guidanceApy` (:3560), silently falls back to an illustrative hardcoded 5.5 — deliberately NOT used.
   The title/tagline render site (:4420) needed no code change — it already reads `t('title')`/`t('tagline')`; only the strings moved. `PlannerHeader` (and its header-icon route into the analytics app) is unmodified.
3. **planner.min.js / translations.min.js** — regenerated via `npm run minify`. plan.html loads the `.min` files, so skipping this ships nothing (the exact bug item 061 caught). `test_minified_assets.js` verifies byte-freshness.
4. **test_hero_copy.js** (new) + **package.json** (test chain append) — Playwright drives the real rendered plan.html via Chromium with fixture-routed `yields.llama.fi/pools` (the sanctioned in-sandbox pattern). The fixture has 3 rail-passing stable pools (5/6/7% APY, TVL ≫ $50M) and 3 decoys that each trip a distinct rail (non-stable symbol, sub-floor $20M TVL, 5000% anomaly). The test independently derives the expected rate through the REAL exported `curatePools` + `blendedApy` (median = 6%) and asserts the rendered hook shows exactly that — proving live derivation, not string-matching a hardcoded number. Failure scenarios (pools route 500 and aborted) assert the exact number-free fallback with no digit-% present. Zero page errors asserted throughout.
5. **specs/068.md** — territory notes appended (blindspot pass findings; the spec's own placeholder invites this).

## Deviations from spec
- Timeframe claim omitted (spec explicitly allows "or is omitted") — see Intuition above.
- Spec said fixture TVL "well above $10M"; the stable band floor is actually $50M (planner.js:533), so the fixture uses $250M–$800M.
- `formatApy` is not on planner.js's exported test `api`; the test replicates its one-line formula with a sync-note comment (rate derivation itself goes through the real exported functions).
