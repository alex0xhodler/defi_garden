# Spec 027: Share-card generator — real service logo + label/headline consistency

## Evidence
Human-reported, with a screenshot of a generated share card (subscription archetype). Two defects in the canvas generator `renderShareImage` (planner.js):
1. **First-row icon is a hardcoded 🌱 sprout**, not the service's real logo. planner.js:2858 —
   `ctx.fillText('🌱 ' + (opts.goalLabel || ''), 110, 170);` — the per-service `icon` domain the data model already carries (`SUBSCRIPTION_LADDER`/`GOALS` entries have `icon: 'spotify.com' | 'openai.com' | …`) is never passed to or drawn by the canvas.
2. **First-row label disagrees with the headline.** In the waitlist card path `doWaitlistDownload` (planner.js ~1600-1620), `headline` is built from the selected-subscription bundle (`dlList` → e.g. "ChatGPT Plus") while `goalLabel:` is computed separately from the plan's top-level `goal` (→ "Spotify"). Screenshot shows first row "Spotify" under a "…covers ChatGPT Plus — forever" headline. The main plan path (~1750) passes `goalLabel: goalLabel(t, goal)` and its headline also uses `goal`, so it's self-consistent — the bug is the waitlist path's two different sources.

## Hypothesis
The share card is the hero viral artifact (standing decision 2026-07-10) — a mismatched label + generic sprout reads as broken/untrustworthy and undercuts the share. Making the first row show the **featured service's real logo + name, consistent with the headline**, makes the card credible and on-brand.

## Decision (human 2026-07-11)
Icon source = **bundle local brand logos** (ship brand icons as local assets / data-URIs, draw those on the canvas). Rationale: no external fetch at share time, no CORS canvas-taint risk, deterministic. Rejected: Google favicon service (not CORS-safe → tainted canvas → `toBlob` throws → share breaks) and per-service emoji (human wants the real logo).

## Change (planner.js + a bundled-logo asset module)
1. **Pick the featured service once.** Define the card's featured service (the hero rung the card celebrates) and use it for the headline, the first-row label, AND the icon — one source, both share paths (`doShareImage` ~1750 and `doWaitlistDownload` ~1611). Fixes the mismatch at root.
2. **Pass the featured service's logo key** into `renderShareImage` (e.g. `opts.brandIcon` = the service's asset key / `icon` domain).
3. **Bundle the logos.** A local module/asset set (data-URI PNG or inlined SVG) for the subscription/goal services in `SUBSCRIPTION_LADDER` + subscription `GOALS`. No network at share time.
4. **Draw on canvas safely.** Preload the featured logo (local asset → not cross-origin, so no taint) and `drawImage` it in the first-row icon slot; **fallback** to the service emoji, then the 🌱 sprout, if the asset is missing/fails — the card must always render and `toBlob` must never throw.
5. Keep everything else (headline/subline/chart/footer/URL-carrying) unchanged.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] First-row icon is the featured service's real bundled logo (not 🌱, not an emoji) for services with a bundled asset; graceful emoji→sprout fallback otherwise
- [ ] First-row label == the service the headline is about, in BOTH the main and waitlist card paths (no Spotify-vs-ChatGPT mismatch) — reproduce the reported case and confirm
- [ ] `toBlob` still succeeds (canvas not tainted) — logos are local assets, loaded before export; a missing/broken asset falls back, never throws
- [ ] Visual check on a REAL render (browser): card looks correct at the export resolution for ≥3 services incl. the reported ChatGPT case; light/dark not applicable (card is fixed-palette)
- [ ] No new external network calls at share time; existing plan-URL-carrying behavior (005) intact
- [ ] `node --check planner.js`; full test chain exits 0

## Measurement
Quality/trust fix on the hero share artifact — supports the north-star share loop but no isolated funnel metric same-week. Do not fake one.

## Territory notes / environment constraint
- **Cannot be built or verified in a no-network / no-browser session:** obtaining brand assets needs network, and the canvas `drawImage`→`toBlob` render is inherently visual (must be eyeballed in a browser). Take this item in a session/run with browser + network. Verifier must reproduce the real render, not just read the code.
- Data model already has per-service `icon` domains (planner.js ~644-692 GOALS, ~720-725 SUBSCRIPTION_LADDER) and `emoji` for fallback. The DOM `brandIcon` helper (planner.js ~1145) shows the favicon pattern used in-app (not reusable for canvas export — favicon service taints the canvas).
- Trademark note: small brand logos used to identify the service on the user's own plan card is nominative identification use; keep them small and unaltered. Prefer official brand-guideline monochrome marks where available.
