# SPEC 261 — Perps & Funding Category Nav & App Integration
**Parent**: Item 261 in `product-loop-kit/BACKLOG.md` | **Risk Tier**: HIGH | **Status**: READY

## 1. Goal
Integrate "Perps & Funding" as a first-class category in `home.html` navigation and search autocomplete, allowing users to browse and search Hyperliquid OIL, Lighter SPCX, HYPE, SOL, and BTC perp funding yields directly in the app.

## 2. Evidence
- On-chain traders search for funding rates and long/short yield conditions alongside lending and staking yields.

## 3. Implementation Plan
1. **Category Tab in `home.html` & `translations.js`**:
   - Add `"navCatPerps": "Perps & Funding"` / `"navCatPerps": "퍼프 & 펀딩"` tab alongside Lending, Staking, LP-DEX, and RWA.
2. **Search Autocomplete (`app.js`)**:
   - Support autocompleting queries like "OIL funding rate", "SPCX perp", "Hyperliquid yields", "HYPE long short".
3. **Acceptance Tests**:
   - `test_perps_app_integration.js`: Assert "Perps & Funding" category tab renders in both EN and KO, and autocomplete resolves perp markets.

## 4. Verification
- `node test_perps_app_integration.js` passes.
- Inspect mounted `home.html` UI verifying the Perps & Funding category tab renders cleanly.
