This is the *targeting* half of the human's stated blocker, measured. (`apyMean30d` is present and non-null
on **405/405** candidates, so the signal this spec adds has full population coverage on live data.)

## Hypothesis

We believe replacing the spotlight ranker's single sort key (headline APY) with a **story-worthiness score
built from the three story types the human named**, gating out targets whose headline is not representative
of their own recent rate, and attaching a **derived one-line hook** to each pick, will make the human
willing to post — because target selection is one of the two blockers they stated, and the current selector
optimises the one quantity ("biggest number") that the product's own positioning says not to lead with.
Confidence: **supported** — the *defect* is measured (table above), the *remedy* is a preference the human
stated in their own words; that the human then posts is a human decision no code can force, and the row
already says so ("Acceptance: the human posts one — that's the real gate, stated honestly").

- **Instance of:** *selectors that rank an outward-facing artifact by a single headline magnitude, with no
  check that the magnitude is representative of the thing it describes.* Population: every ranked/curated
  surface this repo emits. Siblings already ticketed and shipped against the same class: item **032/239**
  (zero-yield rows ranked into the default grid view), item **122/144** (a rendered figure trusted because
  it was under an absolute magnitude bound rather than checked against the rail the product itself
  declares).
- **Class closed by this item: no.** This closes it for the spotlight ranker only — **1 of the ranked
  surfaces** in the repo. The general predicate ("a headline figure may not be surfaced as a
  recommendation unless it is checked against that same series' own recent mean") is **not** enforced
  anywhere else: `generate-token-pages.js`, `generate-chain-pages.js` and the analytics grid's sort modes
  all rank on current APY with no representativeness check. Ticketed as backlog **242** (filed by this
  item's close-out) with the number: **405 candidate pools measured here; the 3 other ranked surfaces are
  unaudited for this class.**

## Change

**In scope — `generate-spotlight.js` only, plus its tests and its regenerated output.**

### 1. Two hard candidate gates, added before ranking (refuse, never demote — `pickPool`'s existing stance)

Both are *additional* filters on top of the existing `isQualifyingPool` (anomaly + $10M TVL) and
`isSmallEnoughProtocol` (Curve ceiling). **No existing rail is relaxed, renamed, or re-valued.**

- **`isRepresentativeRate(pool)`** — `|apy − apyMean30d| <= max(REPRESENTATIVE_REL * |apyMean30d|, REPRESENTATIVE_ABS_PP)`
  with `REPRESENTATIVE_REL = 0.5` and `REPRESENTATIVE_ABS_PP = 0.5` (percentage points).
  `apyMean30d` null / non-finite → **false** (no evidence of representativeness is not evidence of
  representativeness — the artifact is outward-facing and the human's name is on it).
  The absolute-pp term exists so a genuinely flat near-zero pool is not failed by a division-scale artifact
  (`0.02` vs `0.00` is representative; `4.14` vs `0.21` is not).
- **`isFundableForever(pool)`** — the pack's whole premise is a forever number, so a pool whose own
  effective (haircut-applied) rate yields no finite positive `foreverNumber` cannot be a spotlight target.
  Reuses `foreverNumber` from `planner.js` — already imported at `generate-spotlight.js:53`. Never a second
  implementation of the math.

Measured funnel on live data: **15,679 → 405** (existing rails) **→ 369** (representative) **→ 289**
(fundable), across **148** distinct protocols. The ranker's current #1 (`concrete · SRROYUSDC`, 19×
deviation) is excluded by the first gate.

### 2. `storySignals(pool, ctx)` → the score that replaces the APY sort

Four signals, each on `[0,1]`, computed over the **gated candidate set derived at call time** (never a
constant table). Three are **percentile ranks within that set** — deliberately, so that no tuned constant
decides them and the scale is uniform by construction:

| signal | definition | why it exists |
|---|---|---|
| `smallProtocol` | `1 − percentileRank(aggregate railed TVL of pool.project)` | 2026-07-12 decision "Curve is the upper bound, **aim lower in general**" — the existing ceiling is binary; this grades it |
| `unusualRate` | `percentileRank(pool total APY within its OWN persona band's candidates)` | the human's "unusual-but-railed rates". Band-relative, so a 9% stablecoin pool reads unusual and a 25% degen pool reads ordinary — this is **not** a rename of "highest APY" |
| `freshness` | `1 − percentileRank(pool.count)` (`count` = days DefiLlama has tracked the pool) | the human's "fresh listings with ≥$10M TVL"; the TVL floor is already a hard gate, so freshness can never admit a thin pool |
| `rateRepresentative` | `max(0, 1 − min(deviation / REPRESENTATIVE_REL, 1))`, deviation as in the gate | rewards being far inside the honesty gate, not merely past it. **Absolute, not a percentile — it is a property, not a preference** |

`storyScore = mean of the four` — **equal weights, deliberately un-tuned.** Zero packs have ever been
posted, so there is no outcome data to fit weights to; any asymmetry would be a claim stronger than the
evidence (RAZOR.md). This refusal is recorded in the code comment and re-opened when packs accrue outcomes.

`rankCandidates(pools)` sorts by `storyScore` descending; ties break by total APY descending (stable,
documented). `pickPool`'s explicit `--pool <id>` path additionally validates the two new gates and throws
`SpotlightError` with the failing gate named — same refuse-don't-demote contract it already has for the
rails.

### 3. `hookAngle` and the derived one-line hook

`hookAngle` = the highest of **`smallProtocol` / `unusualRate` / `freshness` only**. `rateRepresentative`
is a gate and a score term, **never an angle** — "the rate is what it has been" is a precondition, not a
story. (Measured: restricting the angle this way yields a balanced 130/128/111 split over the 369
representative candidates; including `rateRepresentative` collapsed 229 of 369 onto one template.)

Exactly three hook templates. Every figure in them comes from the pack's own already-railed fields; the
forever clause uses `foreverAmtStr` + `effectiveApyStr` (the haircut-applied basis, per 069) and is
**omitted entirely** when `foreverAmtStr` is null:

- `freshness` — `<ProtocolLabel>'s <SYMBOL> pool on <Chain> has been tracked <D> days and already holds <TVL>. $<foreverAmt> parked there pays your <GoalLabel> forever at <effectiveApy>.`
- `unusualRate` — `<apyStr> on <SYMBOL> is the top <P>% of rates in its risk band on <Chain>. $<foreverAmt> parked there pays your <GoalLabel> forever at <effectiveApy>.`
- `smallProtocol` — `<ProtocolLabel> holds <protocolTvl> across its railed pools — not a household name. $<foreverAmt> parked in its <SYMBOL> pool pays your <GoalLabel> forever at <effectiveApy>.`

Honesty constraints, all verifier-checkable:
- **"has been tracked `<D>` days", never "is `<D>` days old"** — `count` is DefiLlama's tracking window, not
  the pool's age. The pack must not claim the stronger fact.
- No word from CLAUDE.md's in-reach ban-list (`save up`, `afford`, `budget`); no urgency language.
- `<P>` = `round((1 − unusualRate) * 100)`, floored at 1 so it never prints "top 0%".
- For a `degen`-persona pack the existing 069 haircut sentence in `tweetDraft` stays — the hook must not
  displace or contradict it.

### 4. Pack + cadence surfacing (additive only — 069's rule: never rename or delete an existing field)

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `rankCandidates` orders by `storyScore`, not by APY. Proven by a population assertion, not one pool:
      over the derived candidate set, the returned order is non-increasing in `storyScore`, and the order
      is **not** equal to the APY-descending order.
- [ ] `isRepresentativeRate` excludes a pool whose headline deviates from its own `apyMean30d` past the
      threshold, and excludes `apyMean30d == null`. Population test over the derived candidate set: **every**
      pool returned by `rankCandidates` satisfies the predicate — asserted for all, not sampled.
- [ ] `isFundableForever`: every pool returned by `rankCandidates` yields a finite, positive `foreverNumber`
      at its own effective (haircut-applied) rate.
- [ ] `pickPool('<id of a pool failing a new gate>')` throws `SpotlightError` naming the gate that failed —
      never returns the pool, never silently demotes.
- [ ] Each of the four `storySignals` is in `[0,1]` for **every** pool in the derived candidate set, and
      `storyScore` equals their unweighted mean (recomputed independently in the test, not read back).
- [ ] `hookAngle ∈ {smallProtocol, unusualRate, freshness}` for every candidate; `rateRepresentative` is
      never returned as an angle.
- [ ] Every emitted `hook` (a) is one line, (b) contains no ban-list word (`save up`, `afford`, `budget`),
      (c) contains the string "tracked" rather than "days old" when the angle is `freshness`, and (d) when
      it contains a forever figure, that figure equals the pack's own `foreverAmtStr` and the rate it quotes
      equals `effectiveApyStr` — never `apyStr` — asserted over a generated population, both a degen-persona
      and a non-degen fixture.
- [ ] Degen honesty preserved: for a degen-persona pack the 069 haircut sentence is still present in
      `tweetDraft`, and no hook quotes the headline APY as the forever basis.
- [ ] Additive-only: for a fixed pool, every pack field present before this change is still present with the
      same name and same value semantics (asserted field-by-field against a recorded key list).
- [ ] `buildCadence`'s next-candidates list contains no repeated `project`.
- [ ] `spotlights/` holds 3 regenerated packs from **3 distinct protocols**, each with a non-empty
      1200×630 `card.png` and a `pack.json` whose recorded numbers clear the rails (`apy ≤ 1000`,
      `tvl ≥ $10M`) and whose `shareUrl` still carries `goal/monthly/pace/src=x_spotlight/ref`.
      `renderSpotlightCard`'s layout/palette code is **byte-unchanged** in the diff.
- [ ] Tests: assertions live in `test_spotlight.js` / `test_spotlight_packs.js` (extend; do not add a new
      npm-test entry unless a new file is genuinely needed, in which case register it in `package.json`'s
      test chain **and** the test registry that `test_test_registry.js` guards).
- [ ] `node run-tests.js --lane=plain` green, **timeboxed to 5 foreground minutes**; any pre-existing
      failure documented in the notes as pre-existing (reproduced on `origin/main`), never claimed as caused
      or fixed here.
- [ ] **Non-vacuity** — each new rule neutered **separately**, so "four working signals" is distinguishable
      from "one working signal and three dead ones". For each of: `isRepresentativeRate`,
      `isFundableForever`, the story-score sort, the cadence protocol-dedupe — mutate the source to disable
      **only** that rule, show the corresponding test RED, restore, show green, and prove the restore is
      byte-identical (`md5sum` or `git diff --exit-code`). Record all four md5 pairs in the notes.
- [ ] **Population** — every test above draws its population from the pool dataset at test time (the
      committed fixture or `POOLS_FIXTURE`), never from a hardcoded pool id. `concrete · SRROYUSDC` may
      appear **only** as a positive control for the representativeness gate, and the notes must say so.
- [ ] No new dependencies. No trust rail weakened.

## Measurement

Metric: north-star leg (B) is untouched by this item; the channel metric is spotlight-origin funnel activity
(`src=x_spotlight` + per-pool `ref`, already plumbed by item 064).
**Gate:** the window opens only when **the human posts a pack** — the row states this as the real gate, and
no code can open it. Until then this is a **build-leg-only** ship: the deliverable is that three
honest, story-ranked, hook-carrying packs exist for one-tap posting.
**Backstop:** if no pack is posted within **60 days** of ship, the heartbeat closes 229 in LEARNINGS as
`UNEXERCISED — targeting rebuilt, channel never exercised`, and the *next* question is whether the channel
itself is the wrong bet — not whether to re-tune the ranker. Weight re-tuning is explicitly deferred until
posted packs produce outcome data.
**Decision rule:** ≥30 real `src=x_spotlight` events (crawler-classified never count, per
`playbooks/traffic-quality-classification.md`) before any conversion claim.
