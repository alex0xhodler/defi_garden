# Spec 004: Surface the share CTA at the bloom moment

## Evidence
2026-07-10 (Mixpanel 4042048, first active day of tracking): 167 sessions, 5 plan_created, 10 plan_saved (pre-fix), **0 share_link_created**. Instrumentation verified sound (002-notes.md) — so zero shares is a usage gap, not a tracking gap. The north-star funnel (share_link_opened → plan_created) cannot produce its first closure until shares exist.

## Hypothesis
We believe making the share action prominent at the emotional peak — the moment the plan settles/blooms — will produce the first organic share_link_created events, because right now users complete plans without ever engaging the share affordance (0/5). Confidence: guess (no funnel data on CTA visibility yet; do NOT redesign — smallest visible intervention).

## Change
Smallest version: make the existing share affordance visible/prompted once per plan at the bloom/report moment (e.g., surfacing the existing doCopyLink/doNativeShare actions — no new share mechanics, no share-card changes). All copy through translations.js, EN + natural KO together. OUT of scope: share-card PNG redesign, new share channels, referral mechanics, router/URL changes, any trust-rail surface.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Share affordance is visible without scrolling/hunting when a plan reaches its settled/bloom state at 360/768/1280 widths, light + dark, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected
- [ ] No new events invented — existing `share_link_created` (method: copy/native) fires on use (verifier traces the call path)
- [ ] Any new user-facing string exists in BOTH en and ko in translations.js
