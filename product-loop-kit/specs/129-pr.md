# 129 — PR explainer: lead the pool-detail page with the yield-funded frame

Tier: HIGH (render-path change on the pool-detail / north-star surface, user-facing beyond pure copy —
verifier assigns independently; full walkthrough + quiz below per policy).

## Goal
On the pool-detail page — the current north star (`pool_click{source=garden_cta|protocol_link}`) — the first
numbers below the hero were "**Daily earnings $0.10**" and "**Monthly earnings $3**" on a low-APY stable pool
(SUSDS 3.6%). Honest, but they *led* the page and read as underwhelming. Item 129: lead instead with the 5-year
compounded projection and the yield-funded "you keep your money AND get the thing" frame — honest numbers
intact, all trust rails held.

## Intuition
The yield-funded thesis (CLAUDE.md) is: *buy it outright and the money's gone; garden it and you keep the money
AND get the thing.* The number that carries that frame is the compounding projection, not "ten cents a day".
The projection already existed on the page ("The Long Game" card) — it was just sitting BELOW the small-$
earnings grid. So the fix is mostly a **reorder**: put the compelling honest number first.
