   screen of goal chips.

   146 (BACKLOG, 2026-07-26): `mixStats()` resolved any goal with a numeric

   entered — rendered verbatim as `≈$155,000 working at 8.5% · $1,100/mo covered

5. **The planner is the north star's destination.** `NORTH_STAR.md` 2026-07-23:
   the north star is `pool_click{source=garden_cta}` + `{source=protocol_link}`;
   `garden_cta` is "Garden this pool →" (`PoolDetail.js:497`), which lands in the
   planner. The audit confirms that CTA has an `href`; nothing confirms the page
   behind it computes sane numbers.
6. **PRE-TRAFFIC MANDATE** (`NORTH_STAR.md`, 2026-07-23, human directive): with

## Change

### 1. New surface kind `bloom` in `main()`

Loads a deep-linked plan URL and audits the **rendered bloom/checkout screen**.
Checks, all reusing existing helpers and existing detector vocabulary:

- `dead-end` **P1** — `.gp-bloom` / `.gp-checkout-panel` does not render within
  the timeout. (Resolve the real readiness selector by reading `planner.js`
  around `step === 'bloom'` (~4794) and the checkout panel; do not guess.)
- `number-sanity` **P0** — existing `auditText()` over rendered `innerText`.
  This is the check that carries the item: it is the first time
  `scanNumbers()` has ever run against a computed planner number.
- `page-error` **P0** — existing `makeErrorSink()`.
- `dead-cta` **P1** — the bloom screen's primary control (`.gp-checkout-cta`)
  renders and is visible.
- `responsive` — 360 surface only, via existing `checkResponsive()`.
- `i18n` **P2** — `-ko` surface only, reusing the planner branch's existing
  "KO surface rendered no Hangul text" check.

### 3. One interactive check on the existing `planner` surface

The 1280/EN `planner` surface only (not 360, not KO — one flake surface, not
three): after the existing visibility check, **click** the first `.gp-chip` and
assert the planner leaves the goal step within the timeout. A goal chip that
renders but does not advance is a dead CTA on the product's single most
important control, and the current check — visibility alone — cannot see it.
Emit `dead-cta` **P1** on failure. Read `planner.js` to pick a truthful
"advanced" signal (a rendered element that exists only past `step === 'goal'`);
do not assert on internal React state.
