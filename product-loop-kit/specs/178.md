# Spec 178: spotlight packs emit copy + brand tokens for a video template, led by the tracked mean rate

## Evidence
Human direction, 2026-07-29, on seeing `spotlights/pareto-credit-usdc-ethereum/card.png`:

> this is very basic, doesn't spotlight the yield which X wants, keeps the secondary into one of the
> subscriptions, better maybe to show the most stable blended rates from when we are tracking these pools
> than just the live rate? make sure you understand this will be posted as on a template for a twitter post
> video, where i will add animations to make it slick, so maybe even better than images to generate just the
> texts that will be copy pasting, and the branding guidelines to be used for headlines, subheader, apy,
> font colors, family etc

Answers to the three clarifying questions (human, same day): **(1) hero = tracked mean**, **(2) rotate the
subscription**, **(3) one flat block**.

**The rendered PNG is the wrong artifact.** The human composes each post as a *video* in a Canva template
and animates it. A flat 1200×630 PNG cannot be animated, so it is currently discarded work; what is
actually needed is text to paste into template fields, plus the type/colour spec so every post looks like
the same brand.

**The tracked-mean data exists and is real — verified this tick, not assumed.** `data/history/` holds 16
daily files (`2026-07-14` … `2026-07-29`), each an `{poolId: [apy, tvlUsd]}` map. For
`2eb2bdf8-…` (pareto-credit USDC, Ethereum):

| date | APY | TVL |
|---|---|---|
| 2026-07-14 | 8.3132% | $153,539,857 |
| 2026-07-20 | 8.3132% | $159,670,881 |
| 2026-07-25 | 8.3132% | $170,704,205 |
| 2026-07-29 | 8.3132% | $171,597,313 |

**The flat APY is a real observation, not a backfill artifact** — the TVL column moves every day while the
rate does not, so the series is genuinely being re-read. `kpis.apyStdev = 0.00` on this pool is true.
Snapshot KPIs already carry everything needed: `historyPoints` (16), `firstSeen` (`2026-07-14`), `apyMean`,
`apyStdev`, `tvlTrend`.

Across the snapshot, **484 pools** have ≥10 tracked days, ≥$10M TVL and `apyMean > 0`. Ranked by
coefficient of variation (`apyStdev / apyMean`, lower = steadier) then yield:

| project | symbol | chain | mean | stdev | n | TVL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| pareto-credit | USDC | Ethereum | 10.59% | 0.00 | 16 | $15M |
| zerobase-cedefi | USDT | Ethereum | 9.00% | 0.00 | 16 | $14M |
| bitway-earn | USDT | BSC | 8.00% | 0.00 | 16 | $23M |
| aave-v3 | SGHO | Ethereum | 4.25% | 0.00 | 16 | $140M |

And the current pack set is already drifting, which is the second reason to change the output:
`project-x-whype-usdc-hyperliquid-l1` was generated 2026-07-13 at **20.10%**; live today it is **7.85%**.
A spot rate baked into a PNG 16 days ago is a false number waiting to be posted.

## Hypothesis
We believe **leading with the tracked mean and emitting copy instead of a picture will make the packs
actually postable** because (a) the human's bottleneck is filling a video template, which needs strings and
a type spec, not a flat image, and (b) a rate we have watched every day for a known window is both more
defensible and a better hook than a spot rate — *"8.31%, unchanged every day for the 16 days we've tracked
it, while TVL grew $153M → $172M"* is a claim only a tracker can make, and DefiLlama's own screenshot
cannot. Confidence: **supported** — the human stated the need directly and the data was verified present
this tick; what remains untested is whether the posts convert, which no amount of building settles.

## Change

### 1. Hero number = the tracked mean, with its window stated
Replace the pack's headline `apy`/`apyStr` role with a **tracked mean over our observed window**, computed
from `data/history/` (or the snapshot's `kpis.apyMean` where it is already the mean over the same window —
pick ONE source and state which; do not average an average).

Every pack MUST carry, and every copy line that quotes the mean MUST be able to state:
- `trackedMeanStr` — e.g. `8.31%`
- `trackedDays` — e.g. `16`
- `trackedSince` — e.g. `2026-07-14` (from `kpis.firstSeen`)
- `trackedStdevStr` and a plain-language **steadiness** label derived from `apyStdev / apyMean`
  (e.g. `unchanged` at 0, `steady`, `variable`) — the label thresholds live in one exported constant
- `tvlStart` / `tvlNow` over the same window, so the TVL-growth line is available
- `liveApyStr` — kept, as the secondary proof line, NOT the hero

**Honesty rails — non-negotiable, and the reason this item is HIGH:**
- The window is 16 days and the copy must say so. **Never** imply a longer history, never write "always",
  "historically", "since launch", or an unqualified "average" with no window.
- A pool with `historyPoints < 7` is **not eligible** for a pack — there is no mean to speak of. Skip it
  and pick the next candidate; log the skip.
- `APY_SANITY_LIMIT` and the $10M pack floor apply to the mean exactly as they apply to the spot rate.
- The degen ⅓ haircut still applies on top of the mean for degen-persona pools, and the copy still says so
  (standing decision 2026-07-09 / CLAUDE.md).
- If the mean and the live rate diverge by more than a threshold (suggest 15%), the pack is **stale-flagged**
  in its own output so it cannot be posted silently — `project-x` above is the worked example.

### 2. Rotate the subscription goal per pack
`SUBSCRIPTION_GOALS` (`generate-spotlight.js:154`) already holds six: `spotify` $12, `netflix` $18,
`claude` $20, `amazonprime` $15, `disney` $16, `youtubepremium` $14. Today every pack hardcodes
`DEFAULT_GOAL_ID = 'claude'`, so a series of posts repeats one subscription.

Rotate **deterministically** across packs so a run is reproducible and a series does not repeat: index by
position in the run (and/or by a stable hash of the pool id — builder's choice, but it must be stable
across reruns and must be stated in the notes). An explicit `--goal` still overrides. The forever-number
maths is unchanged; only which subscription is named rotates.

### 3. Output = ONE FLAT TEXT BLOCK, plus a brand block
Write `spotlights/<slug>/copy.md` (or `.txt`) — **one flat block the human copy-pastes**, not scene-
segmented, not JSON to be hand-parsed. It contains, in order and clearly labelled: headline, subheadline,
the hero rate, the proof line (window + steadiness + TVL growth), the rotating subscription line, the CTA,
the share URL, and the tweet draft. Plain text, no markup that would paste badly into Canva.

Write `spotlights/BRAND.md` **once per run** (not per pack) with the type + colour spec, read from
`style.css` — never re-typed as literals:
- Font family: `"FKGroteskNeue", "Geist", "Inter", -apple-system, …` (`style.css:93`);
  mono `"Berkeley Mono", ui-monospace, …` (`:95`) **for the rate figures**
- Accent `#3B82F6` (`--color-primary`), deep `#1D4ED8`, ink `--color-slate-900`,
  secondary `--color-slate-500`
- Weights: 400 / 500 / 550 / 600 (`--font-weight-*`, `:106-109`)
- A size ramp for headline / subheadline / APY hero / proof / footnote
- The neumorphic surface tokens if a background is specified — **no gradients, no glow, no off-brand
  one-offs** (standing decision 2026-07-10)

Keep `card.png` as a **secondary** fallback artifact. Do not delete it; do not lead with it.

**Explicitly OUT of scope:**
- The Canva template itself and posting — human-owned (standing decision 2026-07-12).
- Scene/beat segmentation — the human chose one flat block.
- Any change to `planner.js`, the planner's forever-number maths, or the trust rails.
- Any change to which pools are *ranked* as candidates beyond adding the `historyPoints ≥ 7` gate and the
  stability signal.
- Backfilling history further than 2026-07-14. We have what we have; the copy states it.

## Measurement
Metric: prod `page_view` broken down by `src` — currently **one row, `undefined` = 47** over 14 days, i.e.
zero tagged arrivals for 17 straight days. Any non-zero `src=x_spotlight` is the first signal this channel
has ever produced.
Window: **gated on the human's first post**, not on ship — the packs have been ready since 2026-07-13 and
unposted, so a calendar window from ship would measure nothing but the same zero. Read 14 days after the
first `src=x_spotlight` arrival.
Decision rule: this item removes a *blocker to posting*; it cannot move a metric by itself. Judge it on
whether the human posts, not on traffic. Per NORTH_STAR's minimum-sample rule, make no funnel claim below
30 events.

## Risk tier (builder's guess — verifier assigns independently)
**HIGH.** It publishes numbers under the DeFi Garden brand to an external audience, and it introduces a new
*claim class* — a historical average — that no existing rail covers. The tracked-mean copy is exactly the
kind of derived figure items 159/165/174 were filed against. Diff will also exceed LOW's 150-line cap.
Not on the NEVER list: no rail is weakened, and the `historyPoints ≥ 7` gate plus the staleness flag
constrain the claim *more* tightly than the current spot-rate output.
