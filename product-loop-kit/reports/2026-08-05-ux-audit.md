## The one-line diagnosis
The prod screenshots are the **pre-225 state** — three design systems coexisting (marketing landing /
terminal-skin grid / neumorphic pool detail). **PR #393 (item 225, "Quiet") already unifies the
component geometry** — its measured after-state: primary buttons 1 radius (12px) 1 height (40) across
all surfaces, cards 1 radius (16px), inputs 1 radius, chips 1 radius — and it is sitting OPEN behind
the human's own pre-merge screenshot gate. **Reviewing #393 is the single highest-leverage act; nothing
below merges cleanly before it.** What follows is what #393 does NOT fix — the structural and content
layer a CSS reset cannot touch — filed as items 236-241, sequenced ON TOP of the Quiet system.

## Findings that SURVIVE #393 (each verified against the 225 branch, not assumed)

**F1 — Three header/nav STRUCTURES, not three header skins.** Landing: wordmark + "Search yields /
How it works / My garden" + lang/theme. Grid: search bar + category tabs, NO nav links (`grep 'How it
works\|My garden' app.js` = 0). Pool detail: breadcrumb pill "Search Results → USDC Pool" (shown even
on direct arrivals with no search context), NO nav. 225 unified header *styling* (56px, except grid
113px) but a user who leaves the landing loses all navigation — no way back to "My garden" from the
exact surfaces the north-star CTAs live on. → **236**.

**F2 — Pool-detail renders the SAME CTA pair twice.** Hero ("Garden this pool → / Start Earning on
maple ↗") and again mid-page under "Ready to start this garden?" (`PoolDetail.js:1287`,
`repeatCtaHeading`). Two full-strength primary blocks on one page is the 008 anti-pattern (one action
per intent level) on today's north-star surface. Structural markup — untouched by 225. → **237**.

**F3 — The terminal skin survives in the data layer.** On the 225 branch: **11** `text-transform:
uppercase` rules (same count as main), **4** hardcoded `'SF Mono'…` font stacks beside the one
tokenized `--font-family-mono`, `.pool-symbol`/`.pool-context-inline` mono-uppercase badges ("ON LIDO
• ETHEREUM"). The grid rows still read as a different product from the sentence-case landing. Plus the
verifier-flagged `.logo:hover { transform: scale(1.02) }` (`style.css:1644` on the branch) — a banned
scale-pop with no reduced-motion guard. → **238**.

**F4 — The default grid ranks 0%-yield rows into the top 10.** In the human's own screenshot, 3 of the
9 visible "Popular DeFi Yields" rows are `0% — No supply yield` (WSTETH/sparklend $2.4B, CBBTC/morpho
$2.4B, WEETH/aave-v3 $2.3B) sitting ABOVE yield-bearing pools. A yield-finder whose flagship list
leads with no-yield rows reads broken to the cautious saver. Ranking logic, not CSS. → **239**.

**F5 — Brand voice splits by surface.** Landing: "A calmer way to explore yield." Grid/pool footer:
"Made with AI & Degen Love." (`app.js:2989,3541` + `translations.js:140,193` — survives the 225
branch). The ICP is the cautious saver; "Degen Love" is the persona we explicitly do NOT serve, in the
trust position (footer) of the two surfaces where money decisions happen. Footers also differ
structurally across the three surfaces. → **240**.

**F6 — Number-format hard-rule violation, visible on one screen.** Grid header: "1,976 results"
(`app.js:3131`, correctly `toLocaleString('en-US')`) two inches above "1976 pools found"
(`translations.js:36` — `showingResults` interpolates the BARE count). Same number, two formats,
violating the pinned-en-US hard rule. KO variant same class. Weakest form: every count-interpolating
translation goes through the pinned helpers. → **241**.
