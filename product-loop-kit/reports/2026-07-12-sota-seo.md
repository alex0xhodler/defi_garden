## Confirmed gaps (grep evidence, this session)
| # | Gap | Evidence (verified today) |
|---|---|---|
| A | **The entire SEO surface is ORPHANED from the homepage.** | `grep href=".../tokens|chains/" home.html` → **0 hits**. 2,108 pages (2,022 tokens + 86 chains) with no crawl path from `/`. |
| B | **No hub/pillar pages exist.** | No `tokens/index.html`, no `chains/index.html`, no category pillars. Thousands of spokes, zero hubs. |
| C | **The pool tables — the pages' core value — carry no structured data.** | Structured-data types across the surface: only `BreadcrumbList` (87), `ListItem` (261, from the breadcrumbs), `WebSite` (1), `Organization` (1). **No `ItemList`, no `Dataset`.** 040 deliberately deferred this. |
| D | **No GEO/AEO answer content on token/chain pages.** | No FAQ/direct-answer block on any token/chain page (only `stories/kevin.html` has `FAQPage`). Pages open with a data-intro sentence, not a query-answering format. |
| E | **No freshness signals.** | `grep "last updated|dateModified"` on token/chain pages → **0 hits**, despite daily CI regen. Sitemaps emit `lastmod`, but the pages don't advertise freshness on-page or in schema. |
| F | **Token pages don't cross-link to chain pages (or categories).** | `grep href=".../chains/" tokens/1inch.html` → 0. Related-links stay within the same surface (token→token, chain→chain). Weak topical graph. |
| G | **No hreflang / no Korean static pages.** | `grep hreflang tokens/1inch.html` → 0 (story pages have 3). `ko` is a first-class app language, but all 2,108 static pages are English-only, no `hreflang`. |
| H | **One shared `og-image.png` for every page.** | Every token/chain page ships `og:image=.../og-image.png`. No per-page social/SERP imagery. |

## 2026 SOTA rationale (sources at bottom)
- **Orphan pages don't index** and critical pages must be **≤3 clicks from the homepage**; LLM crawlers read internal-link topology as a topical-authority signal (2026 internal-linking consensus). Gaps A/B/F are the highest-leverage — they gate whether any of the 2,108 pages ever earn traffic, which is *exactly* the "Crawled/Discovered — currently not indexed" failure the original GSC diagnosis (spec 010) traced.
- **`ItemList` on ranked/collection pages** → mobile carousel eligibility + an AI-Overview/entity citation signal; the correct schema for a ranked pool list (and the reason 040 rightly avoided `Product` — a collection isn't a single product). Gap C.
- **GEO/AEO**: ~70% of DeFi-category queries resolve in AI answers; first-200-words direct answers + FAQ/definition blocks are prime citation fuel. `FAQPage`'s visual SERP result was retired 2026-05-07 but the markup stays valid and is still consumed by AI engines. Gap D.
- **AI recency bias**: citations to content >3 months old drop sharply; a visible "Last updated" + `dateModified` is a cheap, high-ROI freshness signal — and we already regenerate daily, we just don't advertise it. Gap E.
- **i18n SEO**: `hreflang` on already-translatable content is a low-risk reach expansion. Gap G (larger effort — doubles page count).
