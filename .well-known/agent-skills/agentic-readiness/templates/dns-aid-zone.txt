; =============================================================================
; DNS-AID Well-known Entrypoints Template
; Replace <yourdomain.com> with your apex domain, and <target.yourdomain.com>
; with your primary CDN/hosting endpoint (e.g. www.<yourdomain.com>).
; =============================================================================

; 1. General Discovery (_index._agents)
; Points agents to your LLM index / sitemap and establishes content preferences
_index._agents.<yourdomain.com>.   300 IN HTTPS 1 <target.yourdomain.com>. alpn="h2" port="443" path="/llms.txt"
_index._agents.<yourdomain.com>.   300 IN TXT   "content-signal: ai-train=no, search=yes, ai-input=no"

; 2. Model Context Protocol (_mcp._agents)
; Advertises your MCP Server card and dynamic execution endpoints
_mcp._agents.<yourdomain.com>.     300 IN HTTPS 1 <target.yourdomain.com>. alpn="h2" port="443" path="/mcp"
_mcp._agents.<yourdomain.com>.     300 IN TXT   "mcp-server-card=https://<target.yourdomain.com>/.well-known/mcp/server-card.json"

; 3. Agent-to-Agent Catalog (_a2a._agents)
; Points agents to your API catalogs and OpenID Connect configurations
_a2a._agents.<yourdomain.com>.     300 IN HTTPS 1 <target.yourdomain.com>. alpn="h2" port="443" path="/.well-known/api-catalog"
_a2a._agents.<yourdomain.com>.     300 IN TXT   "api-catalog=https://<target.yourdomain.com>/.well-known/api-catalog"
