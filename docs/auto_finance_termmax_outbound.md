- **Sticky Locked Euro TVL:** Users locking `baseEUR` to fund ongoing monthly expenses rarely pull liquidity during crypto market swings.
- **50/50 Interchange Rev-Share:** Auto Finance captures recurring revenue on all settled European Visa card spend.
