## 1. ICP Definitions & Commercial Profiles

### ICP 1: Web3 Freelancers & Remote Engineers
- **Persona:** Paid in USDC/USDT on Base, Arbitrum, or Solana. Currently pays 1.5–3.0% in off-ramping fees + bank conversion spreads just to pay recurring USD SaaS invoices.
- **Average Spend:** $75 – $150 / month (Cursor Pro, ChatGPT Plus, GitHub Copilot, Cloudflare, VPN).
- **Required Deposit:** $1,500 – $3,000 USDC in a 6–10% APY pool (e.g. Morpho USDC or Ethena sUSDe).
- **Core Value Prop:** "Deposit once in USDC, never off-ramp to pay your dev tools again."

### ICP 2: Web3 Startups & Boutique Agencies
- **Persona:** Small remote teams (3–15 people) holding $50,000 – $250,000 in operational stablecoin treasuries.
- **Average Spend:** $400 – $1,200 / month (Slack, Google Workspace, Notion, AWS, OpenAI Team).
- **Required Deposit:** $10,000 – $30,000 USDC.
- **Core Value Prop:** "Eliminate recurring SaaS burn from your P&L forever while retaining 100% of your treasury principal."

### ICP 3: DeFi Protocols & LST/LRT Issuers (B2B Distribution)
- **Persona:** Growth and liquidity leads at Morpho vaults, Aerodrome, Aave, and Lido.
- **Core Pain Point:** Mercenary capital churning when base APY fluctuates.
- **Core Value Prop:** "Sticky TVL lockup: users who tie their vault deposits to active card subscriptions don't withdraw during market volatility."

## 3. Commercial Validation Interview Questions

1. **For Remote Freelancers:**
   - *How do you currently pay for your USD subscriptions using your on-chain crypto income?*
   - *What is your biggest headache during monthly accounting or off-ramping?*
   - *If you could lock $2,000 USDC once and never receive a SaaS bill again, would you prefer that over manual monthly card payments?*

2. **For Startup Founders / DAOs:**
   - *What is your monthly fixed SaaS tool budget, and what percentage of your stablecoin treasury sits idle in cold storage?*
   - *How does your finance team handle employee software card expense approvals?*
   - *Would you pilot a dedicated $25k team treasury vault to cover your core tool stack?*
