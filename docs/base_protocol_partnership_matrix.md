# Base-Native Protocol Partnership Matrix: Incumbents & Emerging Challengers

## Executive Summary
For Base-native protocols, user acquisition and TVL retention are zero-sum against major L1 exports (Aave, Uniswap). By embedding `<SpendYieldWidget />`, these protocols turn their native yield pools into real-world payment rails, incentivizing users to lock capital long-term without withdrawing principal.

---

## 1. Base-Native Incumbents (High TVL, Scale Distribution)

### 1. Moonwell (`WELL`)
- **Protocol Type:** Base-native Lending & Borrowing Market (Compound v2 fork with Pyth/Chainlink feeds).
- **TVL / Profile:** ~$80M–$150M TVL on Base; core liquidity hub for USDC, EURC, cbETH, and cbBTC.
- **Strategic Fit:** Moonwell USDC supply pools offer steady 4.5%–7.5% net APY. Embedding `<SpendYieldWidget />` creates a dedicated "Moonwell Card Vault" where users supply USDC to pay daily subscriptions, ensuring sticky lending deposits that rarely get withdrawn.
- **Pitch Wedge:** Turn Moonwell into the primary decentralized bank account on Base.

### 2. Seamless Protocol (`SEAM`)
- **Protocol Type:** Native Base Liquidity Market (Aave v3 fork + Integrated Liquidity Strategies / ILMs).
- **TVL / Profile:** ~$30M–$60M TVL; pioneered single-click looping strategies on Base.
- **Strategic Fit:** Seamless already specializes in ILMs (leveraged yield positions). We can partner to power our **Boosted Mode ⚡** directly through Seamless leverage vaults, sharing the 15–20% performance fee with the Seamless DAO treasury.
- **Pitch Wedge:** Seamless powers the leverage backend for automated subscription funding.

### 3. Aerodrome Finance (`AERO`)
- **Protocol Type:** Dominant Meta-DEX & Concentrated Liquidity Layer on Base.
- **TVL / Profile:** >$500M TVL; deepest stablecoin liquidity pools (USDC-USDbC, USDC-EURC, USDC-USD+).
- **Strategic Fit:** Partner with top Aerodrome automated liquidity managers (ALMs like Merkl / Gamma / Steer) to allow LP depositors to sweep trading fee yields into active Visa cards without manual claiming.
- **Pitch Wedge:** "LP on Aero, let trading fees pay your monthly rent and software."

## 2. Emerging Base Challengers & Low-TVL Growth Players (Hungry for Utility)

### 4. Extra Finance
- **Protocol Type:** Community-first Leveraged Yield Farming protocol on Base & Optimism.
- **TVL / Profile:** ~$10M–$25M TVL; active yield farmers seeking high-efficiency leverage on stable pairs.
- **Strategic Fit:** Ideal challenger partner to white-label a high-yield "Turbo Card" tier (10–18% APY on farming pairs) with automated deleverage safety rails powered by @risk-quant.
- **Pitch Wedge:** Immediate utility for Extra Finance farmers to lock in real-world subscription utility.

### 5. Overnight Finance (`USD+`)
- **Protocol Type:** Delta-neutral yield-generating stablecoin native to Base and Arbitrum.
- **TVL / Profile:** ~$5M–$15M on Base; daily rebasing stablecoin paying ~8–12% APY directly to token holders.
- **Strategic Fit:** Perfect 1-click subscription token. Users simply hold `USD+` in their `SpendYieldProxy`, and the daily rebase yield is automatically swept to their Rain virtual card balance with zero transaction fees or complex smart contract interactions.
- **Pitch Wedge:** "Hold USD+, get free AI tools forever."

### 6. Avantis (`AVNT`)
- **Protocol Type:** Base-native Decentralized Perps & Real-World Asset (RWA) synthetics.
- **TVL / Profile:** ~$10M–$20M TVL in the Avantis Liquidity Pool (AVLP USDC vault).
- **Strategic Fit:** AVLP earns 12–25% APY from trader loss/funding/borrow fees. We can offer a dedicated "Degen Subscription Vault" where AVLP yield covers subscriptions, with auto-hedging safety clamps.
- **Pitch Wedge:** Convert speculative trading volume into guaranteed subscription cashflow.
