# Product Feature Spec: Kevin's First Home Down Payment Garden: Solana SOL-USDC (81.23% APY) vs. Base WETH-USDC (77.67% APY)

## Summary
This programmatic story compares two high-yield strategies—Solana's SOL-USDC and Base's WETH-USDC—for a tangible goal like a first home down payment. It illustrates how DeFi Garden helps users navigate multi-chain opportunities to achieve their savings targets efficiently. Users can visualize their principal growing in these specific pools.

## Telemetry Goals
- Track organic search queries for 'Solana down payment yield,' 'Base crypto savings,' 'first home DeFi strategy,' and Umami click-through rates from this story to the main savings planner.
