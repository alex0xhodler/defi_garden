## 1. Value Proposition & Commercial Deal Terms

### What We Offer the Protocol:
1. **Sticky Liquidity Lockup:** Deposits tied to active, recurring card subscriptions have an average holding duration of >9 months, dramatically reducing mercenary capital churn.
2. **Co-Branded Visa Card:** We provision a branded virtual Visa card (e.g., "Moonwell Card", "Seamless Card") with Apple/Google Pay push-provisioning at zero upfront cost to the protocol.
3. **Shared Revenue Split:** 50% revenue share on settled Visa interchange fees generated by the protocol's users, plus fee rebates on backend strategy routing.
4. **Zero Engineering Overhead:** 5-minute integration via our drop-in `<SpendYieldWidget poolId="..." />` web component.
