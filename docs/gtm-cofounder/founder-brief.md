# Founder Brief: DeFi Garden

## Strongest Asset [validated]
Proprietary daily risk-adjusted scoring model for all multi-chain pools with over $100K in capital, updated via daily snapshots.

## 1. What It Does [assumption]
DeFi Garden offers information about yields across many blockchains and tokens, helping users "plant" a garden around those yields to accomplish real-world goals (such as earning $20 per month to pay for a Codex Pro subscription) while keeping their principal intact and paying the subscription from yields via card payment rails.

## 2. Who It Is For [assumption]
- Primary ICP: Crypto-native developers who pay for developer tools and AI subscriptions (e.g. Codex Pro, Claude Pro, compute/APIs).
- Secondary ICP: Onchain crypto users who want to save for specific financial goals using their onchain balances.
- Agentic consumer layer: Software agents that consume yield data and execution tooling programmatically.

## 3. Alternative & Why Us [assumption]
- Current alternative: Using DeFiLlama for yield discovery, manually depositing into lending protocols or farms, and manually off-ramping earnings through neobanks or crypto debit cards using cashbacks and bonuses.
- Why DeFi Garden: Automates the entire goal-to-yield-to-subscription pipeline in a single unified flow, calculating exact capital requirements and routing returns directly into subscription payments without touching principal.

## 4. Stage & Traction [validated]
- Domain age: Active for over 1 year (renewed after expiration).
- Traffic: ~130 users in the last 30 days (entirely organic traffic). Mixpanel tracking active.
- Product state: Pool discovery and yield data exploration are live. The automated "plant -> pay" execution feature is currently in development.

## Evidence & Validation Status
- Validated: Pool explorer is live, ~130 monthly organic users, daily risk scoring snapshot engine running for pools over $100K TVL.
- Assumptions to validate: Target developer willingness to deposit capital for tool subscriptions, automated card/off-ramp demand, agentic yield consumption demand.
