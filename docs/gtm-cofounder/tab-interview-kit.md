# Technical Advisory Board (TAB) Interview Kit: DeFi Garden

## Objective
Validate how crypto-native developers manage onchain capital, pay for SaaS/AI subscriptions, and navigate the friction between onchain yields and fiat expenses without pitching any product.

---

## The 7 Core Questions (Max 30 Minutes)

1. **Magic Wand:** "If you could wave a magic wand and change anything about how you manage and spend your crypto balances for everyday tooling and recurring expenses, what would that look like?"
2. **The Stakes:** "If that friction disappeared tomorrow, how would that change your workflow, cash flow, or treasury management?"
3. **Why Now:** "What has changed in your tech stack or monthly tool expenses in the last 12 to 18 months that makes recurring subscriptions more noticeable or painful?"
4. **Current Workflow:** "Walk me through how you currently pay for your recurring developer and AI subscriptions (e.g. Claude, Cursor, GitHub, cloud compute, APIs). How much comes from fiat versus onchain funds?"
5. **Alternative Workarounds:** "What have you tried so far to make your idle stablecoins or crypto pay for your operating expenses (e.g. lending protocols, crypto debit cards, manual off-ramping), and where did that fall short?"
6. **Cost of Inaction:** "If you keep handling this the current way for the next year, what is the downside or annoyance (e.g. wasted yield, tax headache, manual off-ramp fees, subscription sprawl)?"
7. **Trusted Sources:** "Where do you go to find reliable yield information, new crypto tools, or dev workflow discussions that you actually trust?"

## Target Personas to Recruit (Aim for 10 initial invites)

1. **AI/Crypto Indie Developers:** Builders with multiple subscriptions (Cursor, Claude Pro, OpenAI APIs, Vercel) holding onchain balances.
2. **Web3 Protocol/Agency Engineers:** Devs paid partially in crypto or stables who manage personal crypto treasuries.
3. **Onchain Power Users / Yield Farmers:** Users familiar with DeFiLlama, Aave, and lending pools who also use crypto cards (Gnosis Pay, Holyheld).
