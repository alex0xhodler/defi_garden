Core Capabilities & Trust Rails

DeFi Garden filters raw DeFi data through strict invariant trust rails:

  • $100K Minimum Liquidity Floor: Excludes micro-cap and low-liquidity pools to prevent honeypot and rug-pull risks.
  • 1000% APY Anomaly Cap: Flags and excludes extreme flash-loan or distorted rate spikes from list responses.
  • Search Across 30+ Chains: Instant filtering for USDC, USDT, ETH, WBTC across Ethereum, Solana, Base, Arbitrum, BSC, and more.
  • Garden Planner: Calculates compound wealth trajectories and the "Forever Number" recurring expense coverage.
